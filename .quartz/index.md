# Quest for the Shattered Seal

*"Keep searching. But remember to look up."*

---

This is the campaign journal of **Zephyrix "Zeph" Skydelver**, an Aarakocra Cleric of Oghma, Knowledge Domain. Everything here is written from his perspective. What he knows. What he does not know. What he suspects and what he fears.

He is in a jail cell in Grainfall. Session one begins at dawn.
