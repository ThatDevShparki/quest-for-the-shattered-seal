# The Ruined Observatory

## The Location

At the edge of the forest just outside [[Grainfall]] stands a structure the locals call the Ruined Observatory. [[Zephyrix Skydelver|Zeph]] found it during his first day in the area, cross referencing his father's notes against the local geography while orienting himself before approaching [[The Sealed Ruin Beneath Grainfall|the sealed ruin]] beneath the town.

It sits where the treeline begins, which gives it a quality of existing between two things, neither fully part of the town nor fully part of the forest. Whether this positioning was deliberate when it was built or simply the result of the town growing in the other direction over the centuries, he cannot say. It feels deliberate. Most things that old and specific feel deliberate when you have spent your life being trained to read sites.

He approached it without particular expectation. His father's notes referenced the general area but not this structure specifically, or not that he had found yet. He went in because he always goes in. That is the archaeologist's reflex and he has had it since childhood.

---

## The Structure

It is ruined in the specific way that structures ruin when they are old enough and have not been maintained but have also not been actively destroyed. The decay is natural rather than violent. Walls still standing but worn. Upper levels compromised but present. The bones of whatever it was are intact even where the flesh of it has gone.

The architecture reads as significant to his trained eye. Not a domestic structure, not a military one. Something built for a purpose that required height and specific orientation and a relationship to the sky above it. Whether the observatory designation is accurate, whether this was genuinely built for celestial observation or whether the name is a local approximation of something older and less legible, he does not know. The bones of the structure are consistent with the name. They are also consistent with other purposes he has not yet had time to consider properly.

He spent less than an hour inside. He was moving toward [[The Sealed Ruin Beneath Grainfall|the sealed ruin]] and this was a stop along the way rather than a destination. He saw enough to know he had not seen enough.

---

## The Occupant

Someone is living on the upper levels.

He did not see them directly. He saw the evidence of them, the specific accumulation of signs that distinguishes a space that is used from a space that is merely standing. The kind of reading his father taught him to do before touching anything in an excavation site. You learn to see occupation before you see the occupant.

He did not go up. He was moving toward [[The Sealed Ruin Beneath Grainfall|the sealed ruin]] and the upper levels were compromised enough that ascending would have taken more time than he was willing to spend on a first pass. He noted the evidence and filed it and kept moving.

He does not know who is living there. He does not know how long they have been there or why they chose this specific structure or what their relationship is to what he found on the lower level. He does not know if they were present when he was inside or whether the upper levels were empty during his visit.

He intends to find out. He has not yet had the opportunity.

---

## The Tapestry

On the lower level of the Observatory, hung against one of the interior walls, he found a tapestry.

It was worn and dirty in the way that textiles get when they have been in a damp stone structure for a long time without care, the colors faded and the edges fraying and the surface carrying the particular grime of age and exposure. But beneath the wear the quality of it was apparent. This was not a common object. The weaving was fine and the design deliberate and whatever it had been when it was made, it had been made with intention and skill.

The design on the tapestry bore [[The Dawnmere Lineage|the Dawnmere family crest]].

Not a crude carving, not a scratched marking, not the obsessive repeated drawing of a fracturing mind. A tapestry. Woven. Crafted. Hung on a wall. [[The Dawnmere Lineage|The Dawnmere family crest]] rendered in a textile that suggested this symbol had been displayed here with care, at some point in its history, by someone who considered it worth displaying carefully.

He stood in front of it for longer than he intended to. [[The Sealed Ruin Beneath Grainfall|The sealed ruin]] was waiting and the morning was moving and he had places to be, but he stood in front of that tapestry and looked at it and felt the specific sensation of a world that is larger than you thought it was pressing against the edges of what you knew.

His family's crest. In a ruined observatory at the edge of a forest outside a town he had never visited until three days ago. Hung on a wall by someone, at some time, for some reason he does not yet understand.

He copied down what surrounded it carefully. That is when he found [[The Four Symbols|the four symbols]].

---

## The Four Symbols

For the full clue analysis, see [[The Four Symbols]].

Looking more closely at the tapestry and the stonework surrounding it, he found that what had appeared to be a single symbol was in fact four. Each rendered with the same careful craft as the central crest, each sharing the base structure of [[The Dawnmere Lineage|the Dawnmere crest]] with minor deliberate variations that distinguished them from each other and from his family's symbol.

He copied them all. He recognized them, imprecisely and from somewhere he could not immediately place, from something his mother said while holding [[Sylara's Amulet|the pendant]] out for a small child to look at.

He has not yet had time to study the copy properly. [[The Sealed Ruin Beneath Grainfall|The sealed ruin]] was waiting and everything that followed the sealed ruin left no space for the quiet study that the symbols deserved.

They are in his notes. He is keeping them close.

---

## What It Means That Someone Lives Here

This is the question he has not yet been able to sit with properly.

The tapestry is old. The structure is old. The crest on the tapestry and the symbols surrounding it belong to something ancient. But someone is living on the upper levels right now, in the present, in a ruined observatory at the edge of a forest outside [[Grainfall]] that happens to contain a worn tapestry bearing his family's crest.

That is not coincidence. He does not believe in coincidence of that specific and layered a variety. Someone is in that building for a reason and the reason is connected to what is on that wall and what is on that wall is connected to him and to his mother and to [[The Sealed Ruin Beneath Grainfall|the sealed ruin]] beneath the town and to everything he has come to [[Grainfall]] to understand.

Whether the occupant is connected to [[The Dawnmere Lineage|the Dawnmere lineage]], to [[The Visage|the entity]] in [[The Sealed Ruin Beneath Grainfall|the ceremonial room]], to [[The Orryn Society]], to something else entirely, he cannot say. He cannot say whether they are a threat or an ally or something more complicated than either of those categories covers.

He knows he needs to go back. He knows the conversation he has not yet had in that building may be one of the most important conversations waiting for him in [[Grainfall]].

He has been in a jail cell. He has not had the opportunity.

---

## What He Does Not Know

Almost everything beyond what he saw in less than an hour on a first pass.

He does not know the history of the Observatory, who built it, what it was originally used for, how long it has been ruined, or what its relationship is to [[The Sealed Ruin Beneath Grainfall|the sealed ruin]] beneath the town. He does not know if the two structures are connected historically or architecturally or through the lineage they both bear evidence of.

He does not know who hung the tapestry or when or why this location was chosen for it. He does not know if the tapestry has been there for centuries or was placed recently. He does not know if the occupant of the upper levels hung it or found it already there.

He does not know who the occupant is. He does not know if they know about him, about his family, about why he is in [[Grainfall]]. He does not know if their presence in the Observatory is connected to [[The Sealed Ruin Beneath Grainfall|the sealed ruin]] beneath the town or to the timing of his arrival or to something else entirely.

He does not know what [[The Four Symbols|the four symbols]] mean individually or in relation to each other or in relation to [[The Dawnmere Lineage|the Dawnmere crest]] specifically. He does not know if the tapestry contains information beyond the visual, encoded in the weaving or in the arrangement of the symbols or in something he did not have time to examine properly.

He does not know if it is safe to go back. He is going back anyway.
