# The See Inn

## Why It Matters

This is where [[Campaign Knowledge/Characters/Miss DuBois]] is staying and where she met the party to lay out the quest. It is also where [[Campaign Knowledge/Characters/Mary]] likely works.

---

## What He Knows

A shack, or close to one. The first floor is a food establishment called the Sugar Back Eatery. The second floor is the inn. [[Campaign Knowledge/Characters/Zephyrix Skydelver|Zeph]] went to the second floor to meet DuBois.

The building is modest enough that it does not invite description. It matters because of who was inside it, not what it looks like.

---

## What He Does Not Know

Whether DuBois chose this place or ended up here. Whether it has any significance beyond being where a sick woman is staying while she waits for someone to finish what she could not.
