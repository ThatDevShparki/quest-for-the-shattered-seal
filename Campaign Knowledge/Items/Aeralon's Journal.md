# Aeralon's Journal

![[Resources/Images/Aeralon's Journal - Image.png]]

## The Object

A field journal, worn at the edges and soft with age, its cover a deep brown leather that has darkened further from years of handling. The binding is tight but well used, the kind of book that has been opened and closed ten thousand times by the same pair of hands. A small clasp keeps it shut, simple and unadorned. It smells of dust, old ink, and somewhere underneath both, something faintly mineral, like excavated stone.

[[Campaign Knowledge/Characters/Aeralon Ashdelve|Aeralon]] carried this journal his entire career. To every dig, every archive, every ruin that did not want to be found. It was his most prized possession and his most guarded secret, handled with a quiet reverence he reserved for nothing else. Not for his rarest finds, not for his most celebrated publications, not even for the memories of [[Campaign Knowledge/Characters/Sylara Dawnmere|Sylara]] that he kept in a small wooden box on his desk.

When his son asked about it, and he asked many times with the persistence of a child who senses he is being kept from something wonderful, [[Campaign Knowledge/Characters/Aeralon Ashdelve|Aeralon]] would smile in that distant knowing way of his and say the same thing every time.

_This is your inheritance. And only then will you truly understand its magic._

He never showed him. Not once.

The back half of the journal is untouched. Dozens and dozens of blank pages, cream colored and waiting. [[Campaign Knowledge/Characters/Aeralon Ashdelve|Aeralon]] never filled them. Whatever he was building toward in his final years he ran out of time, or clarity, or both, before he reached them. They belong to [[Campaign Knowledge/Characters/Zephyrix Skydelver|Zeph]] now.

## The First Page

The full account of reading the letter is told in [[Campaign Notes/Story/02 - The Descent|The Descent]].

His mother's handwriting.

[[Campaign Knowledge/Characters/Sylara Dawnmere]], dead twenty years, looked up at him from the page. She had written to [[Campaign Knowledge/Characters/Aeralon Ashdelve|Aeralon]] in the quiet, certain voice [[Campaign Knowledge/Characters/Zephyrix Skydelver|Zeph]] only half remembered. The one that made you feel held without being touched.

> _"We are taught by the ancients. We may dig to discover their truths and learn their secrets. But the purpose is to teach the next generation from their failures and successes. Don't spend your life searching for answers from them while becoming them now. You don't need to leave a message to the next generation. You are with them now. Keep searching. But remember to look up."_

And then, in handwriting that was slightly less steady, added at the bottom in what must have been her final days:

> _"Don't just look up. Point and show Zeph the joys the sky brings."_

This page was private for twenty years. [[Campaign Knowledge/Characters/Aeralon Ashdelve|Aeralon]] never showed it to his son. It was the first thing [[Campaign Knowledge/Characters/Zephyrix Skydelver|Zeph]] read when he finally opened to the beginning, the last page he reached after working backwards through the rest. Everything about his father's life as a father made sense when he read it. [[Campaign Knowledge/Characters/Aeralon Ashdelve|Aeralon]] had not been indulging a curious child on every dig and in every museum and archive. He had been following instructions from the woman he loved, written in the one place he would always return to.

The journal had not failed his father. His father had stopped opening it.

## The Contents — What Zeph Knows

### The Early Pages — Pristine Scholarship

The earliest entries are the work of a young man who has not yet encountered whatever will eventually fracture him. The handwriting is dark and certain. The margins are clean. The ink pressure is even and deliberate.

These pages document field work across multiple excavation sites spanning the early years of [[Campaign Knowledge/Characters/Aeralon Ashdelve|Aeralon]]'s career. The observations are meticulous, the sketches precise and carefully labeled, the cross references thorough. Artifacts are catalogued with the attention of someone who understands that an artifact removed from its context without documentation is a sentence taken from a paragraph, meaningless without what surrounds it.

There are diagrams of architectural features, notes on construction techniques, observations about burial customs and ceramic styles and the metallurgical composition of excavated objects. There are passages in multiple languages, some common, some academic, some that [[Campaign Knowledge/Characters/Zephyrix Skydelver|Zeph]] recognizes from his father's published work and some that appear only here.

This is [[Campaign Knowledge/Characters/Aeralon Ashdelve|Aeralon]] at the height of his craft. A brilliant mind doing exactly what it was built to do, in love with the work, unhurried by whatever is coming.

### The Middle Pages — The Work Continues

The middle section of the journal is where the character of the entries begins to shift, gradually enough that the shift is only visible in retrospect.

The scholarship remains meticulous. The observations remain precise. But [[Campaign Knowledge/Locations/Grainfall]] begins to appear. At first as a reference, a site mentioned in relation to other work, a location cross referenced against other sources. Then as a destination. Then as the destination, the thing everything else is being measured against.

The handwriting is still controlled here. The margins are still mostly clean. But there is an urgency beginning to accumulate beneath the surface of the entries, a quality of acceleration that is not yet visible in the words themselves but is present in the density of them, the way the pages begin to fill more completely, the way the cross references multiply.

[[Campaign Knowledge/Locations/Grainfall]] is pulling him. The pull is not yet pathological. It is still the pull of genuine discovery, the specific excitement of a scholar who has found the thread he has been looking for his entire career.

This is the last section of the journal where [[Campaign Knowledge/Characters/Aeralon Ashdelve|Aeralon]] sounds entirely like himself.

### The Later Pages — The Fracture Begins

The handwriting begins to loosen here. Not dramatically, not all at once, but visibly. The precise columns of field notes start to blur at the edges. Sketches that would previously have been carefully labeled appear without labels, or with labels that do not quite match what has been drawn.

References to old gods begin to appear. Not academically, not as the subject of detached scholarly observation, but with a quality of proximity that is different from anything in the earlier pages. These are not gods being studied. These are gods being encountered.

References to rituals appear alongside them. Specific rituals, cross referenced against texts [[Campaign Knowledge/Characters/Aeralon Ashdelve|Aeralon]] has apparently been consulting, described with the particular attention of someone who is not simply documenting a historical practice but considering its mechanics.

[[Campaign Knowledge/Locations/Grainfall]] dominates these pages. It is no longer one discovery among many. It is the only discovery. Everything else has become context for it.

[[Campaign Knowledge/Organizations/The Dawnmere Lineage|The Dawnmere crest]] begins to appear in the margins of these pages. At first once or twice, drawn carefully and set aside. Then more frequently. Then obsessively.

### The Final Pages — The Fracture Complete

The final pages are nearly illegible. This is the honest description and it does not fully capture what it is like to read them.

The handwriting is the handwriting of a man losing his grip on the instrument in his hand. Not from physical weakness but from something else, something that is affecting the relationship between the thought and its expression, introducing a noise between intention and execution that the earlier pages did not contain. The ink pressure varies wildly. Lines that should be straight are not. Sentences begin and do not complete or complete in ways that do not follow from their beginnings.

Diagrams overlap notes. Notes overlap diagrams. What appear to be attempts at the same symbol are drawn over and over in different sizes and orientations, some careful and some clawed into the page.

[[Campaign Knowledge/Organizations/The Dawnmere Lineage|The Dawnmere crest]] is everywhere in these pages. Drawn obsessively, compulsively, in every available margin and across the faces of diagrams and through the middle of sentences. And every single one is defaced. Crossed out, etched through, lines torn into the page with what must have been fury or fear or something that had stopped being one and become the other.

Surrounding the defaced crests, threaded through the incoherent scribbles, are references to old gods and to rituals and to something ancient and unnamed that [[Campaign Knowledge/Characters/Aeralon Ashdelve|Aeralon]] had clearly begun to understand and just as clearly did not want his son to find.

And then, in the middle of the madness, a single sentence written in perfect clarity. Clean and steady, as though the hand that wrote it had briefly returned to itself from somewhere very far away.

> _"I found her, my love. After 20 years. Sylara is here with me."_

## Sae-th — The Language of the Brothers

Scattered throughout the journal, appearing first in the middle pages and increasing in frequency toward the end, are passages written in a language [[Campaign Knowledge/Characters/Aeralon Ashdelve|Aeralon]] was never able to fully identify or translate.

The script is unlike anything in standard academic catalogues. [[Campaign Knowledge/Characters/Zephyrix Skydelver|Zeph]] has some familiarity with obscure written forms from years at his father's side and recognizes none of what he is looking at. The characters are precise and consistent in a way that suggests a fully developed written language rather than a cipher or a personal notation system. Whatever this is, it was made to be read by people who knew it.

Beside these passages, in handwriting still steady enough to be deliberate, [[Campaign Knowledge/Characters/Aeralon Ashdelve|Aeralon]] wrote a single annotation.

_As far as I can tell this is the language of [[Campaign Knowledge/Clues/The Brothers|the brothers]]. It is called [[Campaign Knowledge/Clues/Sae-th]]. It binds the world._

This annotation raises more questions than it answers. [[Campaign Knowledge/Characters/Zephyrix Skydelver|Zeph]] does not know who [[Campaign Knowledge/Clues/The Brothers|the brothers]] are. He does not know what it means for a language to bind the world, whether this is a metaphysical claim or a literal one or a description of function he does not yet have the framework to interpret. He does not know how much of the [[Campaign Knowledge/Clues/Sae-th]] passages his father was able to translate or whether the annotation represents the full extent of his understanding or merely the part he chose to write down.

The passages themselves remain unread. [[Campaign Knowledge/Characters/Zephyrix Skydelver|Zeph]] does not know [[Campaign Knowledge/Clues/Sae-th]]. He does not know anyone who does.

## The Familiar

[[Campaign Knowledge/Characters/Ari|The raven]] that emerged from the journal in the jail cell in [[Campaign Knowledge/Locations/Grainfall]] was [[Campaign Knowledge/Characters/Aeralon Ashdelve|Aeralon]]'s familiar throughout his career. [[Campaign Knowledge/Characters/Zephyrix Skydelver|Zeph]] did not know this until [[Campaign Knowledge/Characters/Ari]] emerged. He never saw a raven at his father's side. It was never there, or was never visible, or was kept from him by some deliberate concealment he had no reason to suspect.

The journal is where [[Campaign Knowledge/Characters/Ari]] lived after [[Campaign Knowledge/Characters/Aeralon Ashdelve|Aeralon]] stopped being able to maintain the relationship that kept it close. It waited in the only place it had left to wait. It emerged after the ritual in [[Campaign Knowledge/Locations/The Sealed Ruin Beneath Grainfall|the ceremonial room]], in the dark of the cell, and has not left since.

How much [[Campaign Knowledge/Characters/Ari]] knows about the journal's contents is unknown. It speaks in [[Campaign Knowledge/Characters/Aeralon Ashdelve|Aeralon]]'s cadences, in fragments and observations and half finished sentences, but whether this represents actual memory of what [[Campaign Knowledge/Characters/Aeralon Ashdelve|Aeralon]] said and thought or something more limited is not yet clear. It is a source of potential knowledge about the journal and its author that [[Campaign Knowledge/Characters/Zephyrix Skydelver|Zeph]] has barely begun to access.

## A Note on the Blank Pages

The back half of the journal is untouched. Dozens of blank pages, cream colored and waiting.

[[Campaign Knowledge/Characters/Aeralon Ashdelve|Aeralon]] never filled them. Whatever he was building toward in his final years he ran out of time, or clarity, or both, before he reached them.

They belong to [[Campaign Knowledge/Characters/Zephyrix Skydelver|Zeph]] now. Whether he will fill them with answers or questions or something else entirely remains to be seen. What he writes in those pages is his own inheritance to leave behind, when the time comes and he understands enough to write it.

The journal does not grow. It is already complete, or as complete as [[Campaign Knowledge/Characters/Aeralon Ashdelve|Aeralon]] was able to make it before the end.

The blank pages are [[Campaign Knowledge/Characters/Zephyrix Skydelver|Zeph]]'s to fill. The campaign will determine what goes in them.
