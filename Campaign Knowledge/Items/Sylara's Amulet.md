# Sylara's Amulet -- Pendant of Dawnmere

![[Resources/Images/Sylara's Amulet - Image.png]]

## The Object

A simple silver pendant on a worn cord, bearing [[Campaign Knowledge/Organizations/The Dawnmere Lineage|the Dawnmere family crest]]. [[Campaign Knowledge/Characters/Sylara Dawnmere|Sylara]] wore it as her holy symbol throughout her life as a cleric of holy light, channeling her faith through the mark of her own bloodline as though she always knew the two were connected. It was kept for twenty years in a small wooden box on [[Campaign Knowledge/Characters/Aeralon Ashdelve|Aeralon]]'s desk, among the few private things he never spoke about, and passed to his son as part of an inheritance neither of them fully understood.

The pendant is worn smooth from decades of handling. Not damaged, not diminished, simply softened by time and touch in the way that objects become when they are carried by someone who reaches for them often. The crest is still precise, its lines still clean. Whatever [[Campaign Knowledge/Characters/Sylara Dawnmere|Sylara]] was, she took care of what she carried.

[[Campaign Knowledge/Characters/Zephyrix Skydelver|Zeph]] found it in the box after his father died and took it without fully knowing why. It felt like something that should travel with him. He has worn it ever since, using it as his spellcasting focus the way his mother once did, channeling Oghma's domain through an object that was designed for something older and more specific than scholarship. Oghma does not seem to mind. Or perhaps does not yet know.

In [[Campaign Knowledge/Locations/The Sealed Ruin Beneath Grainfall|the ceremonial room]] beneath [[Campaign Knowledge/Locations/Grainfall]], standing before a royal bloodline carved into stone that predated recorded history, he saw [[Campaign Knowledge/Organizations/The Dawnmere Lineage|the Dawnmere crest]] woven into the web of an ancient civilization's royal lineage. He looked down at the pendant around his neck and then back at the wall and understood for the first time that his mother knew exactly where she came from. She wore the mark anyway. She chose light anyway.

He has not taken it off since.

Then came the ritual. [[Campaign Knowledge/Characters/The Visage|The voice]]. It spoke in his mother's sound without her warmth. It named something called Dreamrot without explaining it. And when it spoke of the pendant around his neck, when it said the amulet held more than what he knew, the pendant grew warm against his chest for the first time. Not hot. Warm. Like a hand on a shoulder in a dark room.

He spent the night in the cell turning it over in the dark. Testing what he had felt. Watching it catch light that was not there. [[Campaign Knowledge/Characters/Ari|The raven]] sat on his knee and said nothing useful and he kept turning the pendant over and over until the shape of what it was began to come clear.

It was not simply his mother's holy symbol. It had been waiting. For the ritual, for the room, for him specifically, for a moment when something ancient recognized the crest it bore and responded to it. He does not yet know what responded or why. He knows the pendant is awake in a way it was not before. Patient and unhurried in the way that very old things are patient when they have been waiting for a specific moment and recognize that it has finally arrived.

The crest on its face catches light at angles that do not correspond to any light source in the room. He has stopped being surprised by this.

His mother wore this and chose light. He is wearing it now and something is asking him to choose. He has not answered yet. But the pendant is warm against his chest and growing warmer, and he suspects the question is not going away.

Its full nature reveals itself slowly. This is only the beginning.

## Background

### Sylara and the Crest

[[Campaign Knowledge/Characters/Sylara Dawnmere|Sylara]] wore this pendant her entire life as a cleric of holy light. She described its features to [[Campaign Knowledge/Characters/Zephyrix Skydelver|Zeph]] when he was very young, crouching to his level and holding it out so he could look while she spoke. He does not remember the content of what she said with perfect clarity. He remembers the feeling of the conversation and the quality of her attention when she explained something she considered important. The details of what the crest's features meant to her family are somewhere in him, imprecise and fading, but present enough that he recognized the symbols at [[Campaign Knowledge/Locations/The Ruined Observatory]] when he saw them.

She never explained the deeper meaning of the lineage the crest represented. Whether this was because there was nothing deeper to explain, because she was protecting him, or because she intended to explain it eventually and ran out of time, he does not know. What he knows is that she wore it as a holy symbol of light, which is itself a statement. You do not wear the mark of a bloodline as a symbol of the thing you have chosen over it unless the choosing was deliberate and the mark is meant as evidence of the choice.

### Aeralon and the Box

After [[Campaign Knowledge/Characters/Sylara Dawnmere|Sylara]] died, [[Campaign Knowledge/Characters/Aeralon Ashdelve|Aeralon]] kept the amulet in a small wooden box on his desk alongside the private things he never spoke about. It was not hidden. It was simply not offered. [[Campaign Knowledge/Characters/Zephyrix Skydelver|Zeph]] knew it was there in the way children know about the things their parents keep private, and understood without being told that it did not belong to a question.

[[Campaign Knowledge/Characters/Aeralon Ashdelve|Aeralon]] never wore it, never displayed it, never referenced it in conversation. He kept it close for twenty years without explanation. Whether this was grief, protection, or something more specific, [[Campaign Knowledge/Characters/Zephyrix Skydelver|Zeph]] cannot say.

### The Morning He Left

The morning [[Campaign Knowledge/Characters/Zephyrix Skydelver|Zeph]] read his mother's note and looked up through the dormer and saw the sunrise, he reached for the wooden box. He took the amulet without fully knowing why. It felt like something that should travel with him. He put it on and left for [[Campaign Knowledge/Locations/Grainfall]] and has not taken it off since.

He began using it as his spellcasting focus immediately and naturally, the way his mother had. It responded without friction. He took this as confirmation that the object was simply a vessel for whatever faith the bearer brought to it.

He is less certain of this interpretation now.

### The Activation

For the narrative account of the activation, see [[Campaign Notes/Story/04 - The Ritual|The Ritual]].

In [[Campaign Knowledge/Locations/The Sealed Ruin Beneath Grainfall|the ceremonial room]], when [[Campaign Knowledge/Characters/The Visage|the voice]] spoke of the pendant and what slept inside it, the amulet grew warm against his chest for the first time in his life. He had worn it for weeks by that point and it had been an object, present and meaningful but inert. At the sound of its own nature being named, it became something else. Something that was responding rather than simply being worn.

He spent the night in the cell learning the edges of it. By morning he understood that it was not simply his mother's holy symbol. It had been waiting for a specific moment and the ritual in that room had apparently been enough to constitute that moment.

He does not know what recognized the crest. He does not know what the warmth means or what it is the beginning of. He knows the amulet is awake and that its waking is connected to something in that room and something in his blood.

## The Crest

[[Campaign Knowledge/Organizations/The Dawnmere Lineage|The Dawnmere family crest]] is the central feature of the pendant. Its specific design elements are what [[Campaign Knowledge/Characters/Sylara Dawnmere|Sylara]] described to [[Campaign Knowledge/Characters/Zephyrix Skydelver|Zeph]] as a child. He carries an imprecise memory of that description which was precise enough to allow him to recognize the symbols at [[Campaign Knowledge/Locations/The Ruined Observatory]] as related.

At the observatory he discovered that what appeared to be a single symbol was in fact four, each remarkably similar to the others with minor deliberate variations between them. His family's crest is one of [[Campaign Knowledge/Clues/The Four Symbols|the four]]. He does not yet know which, or what the other three represent, or who carries them.

The crest appears on the walls of [[Campaign Knowledge/Locations/The Sealed Ruin Beneath Grainfall|the sealed ruin beneath Grainfall]], woven into the royal bloodline of a civilization that predates recorded history. It appears in [[Campaign Knowledge/Locations/The Ruined Observatory]] at the edge of the forest. It appears obsessively and defaced in the final pages of his father's [[Campaign Knowledge/Items/Aeralon's Journal|journal]]. It is around his neck right now.

Whatever the crest is the mark of, it is ancient and it is specific and it has been waiting in several places at once for a very long time.

## Mechanics

_Wondrous Item -- Requires Attunement_ _Functions as a Holy Symbol and Spellcasting Focus_ _Tier 1 -- Upgrades throughout the campaign_

### Attunement

You did not choose to attune to this item. It chose the moment for you. In the chaos of the ritual beneath [[Campaign Knowledge/Locations/Grainfall]], with something ancient speaking in your mother's voice and the walls coming down around you, the pendant grew warm against your chest for the first time. Not hot. Warm. Like a hand on a shoulder.

You spent the night in a cell turning it over in the dark, testing what you had felt, watching it catch light that was not there. By morning you understood that it was not simply your mother's holy symbol. It had been waiting for something to wake it. The ritual in that room was apparently enough.

While attuned, the amulet functions as a holy symbol and spellcasting focus. It responds to divine magic and to something older than divine magic, though the bearer may not yet feel the difference.

The crest on its face occasionally catches light at angles that do not correspond to any light source in the room. The bearer notices this more frequently over time.

### Tier 1 Properties

**While in Sunlight**

- Add +PB to one healing roll per turn.
- When you deal radiant damage, add +PB damage once per turn.

**While in Storm Conditions**

- Gain +10 ft movement speed.
- Once per turn when you deal spell damage, add +PB lightning or thunder damage.

**Heaven's Balance (1/Long Rest)**

- When you cast a spell, you may maximize one damage or healing die.

### Design Notes

The Tier 1 properties map precisely onto [[Campaign Knowledge/Characters/Zephyrix Skydelver|Zeph]]'s inheritance from both parents.

Sunlight properties connect to [[Campaign Knowledge/Characters/Sylara Dawnmere|Sylara]]. A cleric of holy light who wore this crest as a symbol of her chosen faith, who looked upward, who wrote to her husband to remember to look up and show their son the sky. The healing and radiant bonuses are her domain expressed through the object she carried.

Storm properties connect to the Aarakocra side of his heritage. Creatures of wind and sky, built for flight, native to the Elemental Plane of Air. The movement and lightning bonuses are his nature expressed through the same object.

Heaven's Balance sits between both. A single moment of perfect maximized intention once per long rest. His mother's faith and his father's blood meeting in one decision.

The warlock arc will change the character of these properties as [[Campaign Knowledge/Characters/Zephyrix Skydelver|Zeph]] moves toward his pact. The amulet is the intended focus for that transition. What it becomes when he answers the question [[Campaign Knowledge/Characters/The Visage|the voice]] asked him will be reflected in what the item unlocks.

## What He Does Not Know

He does not know what the amulet was designed for originally. He does not know what [[Campaign Knowledge/Characters/Sylara Dawnmere|Sylara]] understood about its nature when she wore it, whether she knew it was something more than a holy symbol or whether she wore it in deliberate ignorance of its deeper function.

He does not know what recognized the crest in [[Campaign Knowledge/Locations/The Sealed Ruin Beneath Grainfall|the ceremonial room]] or what the recognition set in motion. He does not know if the warmth he felt was the amulet responding to [[Campaign Knowledge/Characters/The Visage|the entity]] that spoke to him, or to something in his own blood, or to something in the room itself that had nothing to do with either.

He does not know what the other three symbols represent or who carries them or whether they have objects of their own that correspond to his pendant.

He does not know what the amulet will become when he answers the question [[Campaign Knowledge/Characters/The Visage|the voice]] asked him. He suspects the answer will change it. He suspects he is not ready to find out.

He is wearing it anyway. He has not taken it off since [[Campaign Knowledge/Locations/Grainfall]]. He does not intend to.

His mother wore this and chose light. Whatever the amulet is and whatever it is becoming, she made her choice in it and he is going to make his.

He just has not made it yet.
