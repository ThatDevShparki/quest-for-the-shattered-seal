# The Four Symbols

## The Discovery

At the Ruined Observatory at the edge of the forest outside Grainfall, going further inside after noticing someone appeared to be living on the upper levels, Zeph found the Dawnmere family crest carved into the stone.

He stopped to copy down the markings surrounding it. Looking at what he had copied, he realized that what had appeared to be a single symbol was in fact four. Each remarkably similar to the others. Minor deliberate variations between them, specific enough to be intentional rather than the natural drift of repeated carving by different hands over time.

Someone designed these four symbols to be related and distinct simultaneously. The relationship and the distinction are both meaningful. He does not yet know how.

---

## The Recognition

He recognized them.

Not clearly. Not with the confidence of someone who has studied something and can now identify it on sight. With the specific imprecision of a memory that was laid down very early and has never been fully retrieved, the kind of recognition that arrives before the explanation for it does.

His mother described the features of the Dawnmere crest to him when he was very young. She held the pendant out so he could look while she spoke. He does not remember her words. He remembers the quality of her attention and the feeling of being shown something she considered important and the shape of the pendant catching the light while she talked.

Whatever she described to him that day is somewhere in him still. Imprecise and fading but present enough that standing in the Ruined Observatory looking at four carved symbols he had never consciously seen before, something in him knew them.

He does not yet know what the knowing means or what his mother actually told him or whether what he recognized was his family's symbol specifically or something about the relationship between all four.

---

## What He Knows About the Symbols

His family's crest is one of the four. This he is certain of. The Dawnmere crest on his mother's pendant, the same crest woven into the royal bloodline on the walls of the sealed ruin beneath Grainfall, appears here as one variation among four.

The four symbols share a common base. Whatever the original form is, these four are derived from it, each carrying the shared structure with deliberate minor modifications that distinguish them from each other. The modifications are not decorative. They feel functional. Like the difference between four words that share a root but mean different things.

He copied the inscription by hand. He has that copy. He has not yet had the time or the context to study it properly.

Beyond this he knows almost nothing.

---

## What He Does Not Know

He does not know what the four symbols represent individually or collectively. He does not know what the differences between them signify or whether the differences are the point or whether the shared structure is.

He does not know which of the four is his family's specifically beyond the recognition that the Dawnmere crest is among them. He does not know what the other three are the mark of or who carries them or whether those people are living or known or findable.

He does not know the relationship between the four symbols and the Sae-th language his father found in the ruins. He does not know if the brothers his father referenced in the journal annotation are connected to the four symbols or whether that connection is his own inference rather than an established fact.

He does not know what his mother actually said about the crest when she described it to him. He knows she said something meaningful. He cannot retrieve it precisely enough to use it yet.

He does not know who carved the symbols into the Ruined Observatory or when or why that specific location was chosen. He does not know if the person living on the upper levels of the Observatory has anything to do with the symbols or is simply using the structure as shelter.

He does not know if there are other locations where the four symbols appear together. He has found them in two places now, the sealed ruin and the Observatory. He suspects two is not the complete count.

---

## What He Feels

The recognition unsettled him in a way that the crest on the wall of the ceremonial room did not, or not in the same way. The crest in the ruin was a shock. A single symbol in a place it should not have been, enormous in its implication, connected immediately to the journal and to his father's defaced drawings and to everything he had come to Grainfall to understand.

The four symbols at the Observatory were something quieter and in some ways stranger. Not a shock but a resonance. The feeling of a string being plucked that he did not know was strung. Something his mother put in him when he was too young to know it was being put there, surfacing now in a ruined building at the edge of a forest because the right object was in front of him at the right moment.

He does not know what she gave him that day. He knows she gave him something. He knows it is still there waiting to be useful and that the campaign is going to tell him what it actually is.

He copied the inscription carefully. He is keeping the copy close.
