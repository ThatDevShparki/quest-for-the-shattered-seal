# Dreamrot

## The Source

A single word, spoken by [[Campaign Knowledge/Characters/The Visage]] in [[Campaign Knowledge/Locations/The Sealed Ruin Beneath Grainfall|the ceremonial room]] during the ritual. The narrative account of this moment is told in [[Campaign Notes/Story/04 - The Ritual|The Ritual]].

The voice said it without explanation. Dreamrot. Placed into the room without context or ceremony, as though he should already know its weight. As though it should already mean something to him. As though it had been part of his story longer than he had known.

He did not recognize it.

---

## What It Says

Nothing. A word. No definition, no context, no elaboration. [[Campaign Knowledge/Characters/The Visage|The Visage]] named it alongside what it knew of his father and his mother's secrets, threading it into the same fabric of knowledge it offered in exchange for patronage. The word arrived in the company of things that mattered. That proximity is itself a kind of meaning.

He did not feel it land. Not in the moment. The voice was too large and the room was too full and the word passed through him alongside everything else and was gone.

It came back later. In the cell, in the quiet, the word surfaced and would not go away. Not with the force of something demanding attention. With the patience of something that had always been there and was simply waiting to be noticed. It settled into the place where unease lives, the low hum beneath thought that tells you something is wrong before you have evidence for it.

He has not said the word aloud. He is not sure he wants to hear it in his own voice.

---

## What He Has Connected

He has not built a framework for this word. He does not have enough to build one. But the shape of what he fears is forming whether he looks at it or not.

The voice said it in the same breath that it spoke of [[Campaign Knowledge/Characters/Aeralon Ashdelve|his father]]. It said it knew what Aeralon had carried and what carrying it had cost him. And then it said Dreamrot. The sequence may have been deliberate. The sequence may have been the point.

He has been noticing changes in himself. Small things. Familiar things. The same pattern he watched for years in someone else's face before he had the language to name it. He named those changes in [[Campaign Notes/Story/02 - The Descent|The Descent]], before he ever heard the word Dreamrot, before he ever stood in [[Campaign Knowledge/Locations/The Sealed Ruin Beneath Grainfall|the ceremonial room]].

He suspects the word and the changes are connected. He suspects the word names something that happened to his father and is beginning to happen to him. He is not ready to look at that thought directly. He is aware it is there, the way you are aware of a shape in a dark room that you have decided not to turn the lamp toward yet.

---

## What He Does Not Know

Everything that matters.

He does not know what Dreamrot is. Whether it is a disease, a curse, a process, a name for something the world does to certain people. He does not know if it is natural or inflicted. He does not know if it has a source or if it is something that simply exists, like weather, present everywhere and touching whoever it touches.

He does not know if it is what killed [[Campaign Knowledge/Characters/Aeralon Ashdelve|his father]]. He suspects this. He cannot confirm it. The word arrived from a voice he does not trust, in a room he fled, during an encounter he has not finished processing. The source makes the information suspect. But suspect is not the same as wrong.

He does not know if it is what he is beginning to feel in himself. The changes he noticed before leaving for [[Campaign Knowledge/Locations/Grainfall]] are real. Whether they have a name, and whether that name is this one, he cannot say.

He does not know why [[Campaign Knowledge/Characters/The Visage]] named it. Whether the naming was a warning, a diagnosis, a demonstration of knowledge, or the first move in a negotiation he ran from before it could complete. He does not know if the word was offered as a kindness or as a hook.

He does not know if it can be stopped.
