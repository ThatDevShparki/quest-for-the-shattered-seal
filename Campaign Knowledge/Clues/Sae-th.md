# Sae-th

## The Discovery

Scattered throughout [[Aeralon's Journal|Aeralon's journal]], appearing first in the middle pages and increasing in frequency toward the end, are passages written in a script [[Zephyrix Skydelver|Zeph]] does not recognize.

He has some familiarity with obscure written forms from years at his father's side in archives and excavation sites. He recognizes none of what he is looking at. The characters are precise and internally consistent in a way that distinguishes them from a cipher or a personal notation system. Whatever this script is, it was made to be read by people who knew it. It is a fully developed written language that simply does not appear in any academic catalogue [[Zephyrix Skydelver|Zeph]] has encountered.

His father spent years trying to decode it. The evidence of that effort is visible in the margins surrounding the passages, in the cross references and the attempted transliterations and the fragments of partial understanding that accumulate and then break off without resolution. [[Aeralon Ashdelve|Aeralon]] was brilliant and meticulous and he could not crack it. Or could not crack it fully. Or cracked enough of it that what he found made him stop trying to crack the rest.

Beside the passages, in handwriting still steady enough to be deliberate, he left a single annotation.

_As far as I can tell this is the language of [[The Brothers|the brothers]]. It is called Sae-th. It binds the world._

---

## The Annotation

Three claims in one sentence. Each one larger than the last.

**It is called Sae-th.** A name. Which means someone named it, which means someone knew it well enough to give it a name, which means it was not always lost. Something called Sae-th existed in a context where it had a name and presumably speakers and a purpose. That context is gone now or hidden or both.

**It is the language of [[The Brothers|the brothers]].** [[The Brothers]] are unnamed. This is either because [[Aeralon Ashdelve|Aeralon]] did not know their names or because he knew them and chose not to write them down, which are very different things. Brothers implies a relationship, a plurality, a shared origin. Four brothers. [[The Four Symbols|Four symbols]]. [[Zephyrix Skydelver|Zeph]] has not yet committed to this connection as established fact but he has not been able to stop thinking about it since he made it.

**It binds the world.** This is the claim he does not know what to do with. Not describes the world. Not records the world. Binds it. A language that does something rather than simply means something. Whether this is metaphysical, literal, functional, or some combination of all three, he has no framework for yet. He knows his father believed it enough to write it as fact rather than theory.

[[Aeralon Ashdelve|Aeralon]] was not a man who wrote things as fact without basis. He was meticulous about the distinction between what he knew and what he suspected. He wrote this as a statement. That means something.

---

## The Passages

The Sae-th passages in [[Aeralon's Journal|the journal]] are unread. [[Zephyrix Skydelver|Zeph]] cannot read them. He does not know anyone who can.

He has looked at them long enough to notice certain things. The script appears consistent across all instances, suggesting a single language rather than multiple related systems. The passages in the earlier pages of the journal are shorter and surrounded by more annotation, the marks of active decoding effort. The passages in the later pages are longer and the annotations around them are thinner, which could mean his father had decoded enough to stop annotating or had lost the ability to annotate properly or had stopped trying for reasons that had nothing to do with his understanding.

He does not know what the passages say. He does not know if they are transcriptions of something [[Aeralon Ashdelve|Aeralon]] found carved somewhere, original writing by [[Aeralon Ashdelve|Aeralon]] in a language he was learning, translations of something else, or something else entirely.

He has copied several of the clearer passages into his own notes. He is keeping them. He does not know yet what he will do with them or who might be able to read them.

---

## The Connection to the Four Symbols

He has not established this as fact. He is thinking about it.

[[The Four Symbols]] at [[The Ruined Observatory]] are variations of a shared base form. [[Aeralon Ashdelve|Aeralon]] wrote of [[The Brothers|the brothers]], plural, connected to a language that binds the world. Four symbols. Brothers. A language connected to something ancient enough to predate recorded history.

He is aware that he might be connecting things that do not connect. He is also aware that in his experience of archaeological work, the connections that feel like reaching tend to be the ones that turn out to be real. His father taught him that the most important question is usually the one that seems too large to ask.

He is asking it quietly and keeping the answer provisional until he has more to work with.

---

## What He Does Not Know

Almost everything.

He does not know what Sae-th actually says in the passages his father copied or transcribed or wrote. He does not know how much of it his father successfully decoded or whether the decoding cost him something beyond time and effort.

He does not know who [[The Brothers|the brothers]] are. He does not know if they are historical figures, divine entities, something else entirely, or all of those things simultaneously in the way that ancient civilizations tended to make no distinction between the three.

He does not know what it means for a language to bind the world. He does not know if the binding is intact or broken or conditional or ongoing or concluded. He does not know if Sae-th being spoken or written or decoded has consequences beyond the intellectual. He does not know if his father's attempt to decode it contributed to what happened to him.

He does not know if there are living speakers or readers of Sae-th. He does not know if [[The Visage|the entity]] in [[The Sealed Ruin Beneath Grainfall|the ceremonial room]] spoke it or speaks it or is connected to it. He does not know if [[The Four Symbols|the four symbols]] are characters from Sae-th or related to it or entirely separate from it.

He does not know if he should be trying to read it. He does not know if trying to read it is safe.

He is going to try to read it anyway. He is his father's son. The not knowing is always worse than the knowing, or at least that is what he has believed his entire life. [[Grainfall]] is beginning to complicate that belief.

He is still going to try.

---

## What He Feels

Something between fascination and dread, which is a combination he recognizes from his father's best work and his father's worst outcomes.

The fascination is straightforward. A language that predates any known civilization, connected to something ancient enough to have built the ruins beneath [[Grainfall]], carrying a name and a description that suggest it does something rather than simply means something. This is exactly the kind of discovery his father spent his career reaching for. [[Zephyrix Skydelver|Zeph]] inherited the reaching.

The dread is newer and less comfortable. His father found this language and annotated it and kept going back to it and the pages around it grew more frantic rather than more clear. Whatever [[Aeralon Ashdelve|Aeralon]] understood about Sae-th it did not resolve into the clean satisfaction of a problem solved. It resolved into the final pages of [[Aeralon's Journal|the journal]].

He is holding both of those things at once. The pull toward it and the knowledge of where the pull led his father. He is trying to hold them with enough tension to stay honest about both without letting either one make the decision for him.

He is not sure he is succeeding. He is paying attention to the trying.
