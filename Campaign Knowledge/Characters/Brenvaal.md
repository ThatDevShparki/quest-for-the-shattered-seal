# Brenvaal

## Who He Was

Brenvaal was an Aarakocra artisan, a potter and smith, who came to study under [[Campaign Knowledge/Characters/Aeralon Ashdelve|Aeralon]] not as a scholar but as a craftsman. He wanted to understand the ancient techniques, the methods of civilizations that had been building and firing and forging for centuries before the current world had developed its shortcuts. Aeralon had the historical knowledge he needed. The arrangement suited them both.

He was young when he arrived. Not young in years exactly, but young in the particular way that certain people are young, an energy and a lightness that did not read as immaturity but as genuine enthusiasm for being alive and present in whatever room he happened to be in. This quality was not directed primarily at the scholarship. It was directed at everything equally, at the work and the meals and the long evenings of conversation and at the small child who lived in the house and needed someone to get on the floor with him.

He got on the floor. This is the thing [[Campaign Knowledge/Characters/Zephyrix Skydelver|Zeph]] remembers most clearly and most specifically about Brenvaal. Whatever else was happening in the household, whatever [[Campaign Knowledge/Characters/Aeralon Ashdelve|Aeralon]] was working through in the early years after [[Campaign Knowledge/Characters/Sylara Dawnmere|Sylara]] died, Brenvaal got on the floor. He played with Zeph. He ate with him. He treated him not as an inconvenience to be managed around the edges of adult life but as a person worth paying attention to, which is not nothing when you are very young and the most important adult in your life is still learning how to be present.

He set up a small pottery studio in his room in the family home. [[Campaign Knowledge/Characters/Zephyrix Skydelver|Zeph]] remembers this with unusual specificity, the particular smell of clay and the heat of the small kiln and the sound of the wheel, which was rhythmic in a way that was easy to fall asleep to if you were sitting nearby watching someone work. Brenvaal let him watch. More than that, he explained what he was doing, not in the pedagogical way of someone conducting a lesson but in the natural way of someone who thinks aloud and does not mind an audience. Zeph learned the basic vocabulary of pottery without intending to. The names of things. The logic of them. The relationship between pressure and shape and heat and time.

On dig sites and during long field evenings he and [[Campaign Knowledge/Characters/Aeralon Ashdelve|Aeralon]] collaborated in the care of [[Campaign Knowledge/Characters/Zephyrix Skydelver|Zeph]] the way two people collaborate on anything when neither of them is entirely certain what they are doing but both of them are committed to doing it. Aeralon taught. Brenvaal entertained. Between them they covered most of what a small child needs from the adults in his vicinity. Zeph did not understand at the time that this was a system. He understood it only as the texture of his daily life, which included his father's voice explaining things and Brenvaal's presence making the explaining feel less like instruction and more like conversation.

## The Leaving

He left when he had learned what he had come to learn. This is the clean version and [[Campaign Knowledge/Characters/Zephyrix Skydelver|Zeph]] believes it is essentially accurate. The arrangement had a natural end point and when it arrived Brenvaal moved toward it, which is the correct thing to do and the sign of a relationship that was healthy rather than dependent.

There was no falling out. [[Campaign Knowledge/Characters/Zephyrix Skydelver|Zeph]] is certain of this. The departure was not the product of conflict or disappointment or anything that needed to be repaired afterward. Brenvaal and [[Campaign Knowledge/Characters/Aeralon Ashdelve|Aeralon]] remained in loose contact for years, the kind of contact that consists of occasional correspondence and the mutual knowledge that the other person is out there doing well, which is its own form of friendship even when it produces nothing tangible.

Zeph was young enough when Brenvaal left that the leaving did not feel like loss at the time. It felt like a chapter ending, which children accept more readily than adults because they have less basis for comparison. He simply stopped being there one day and life continued without him in it.

What Zeph carries from that period is the warmth of it rather than the detail of it. The feeling of a household that was grieving but not paralyzed. The sense that someone had decided he was worth paying attention to. The smell of clay. The sound of the wheel.

## The Closed Door

The last thing [[Campaign Knowledge/Characters/Zephyrix Skydelver|Zeph]] knows about Brenvaal is a conversation he was not part of.

He does not know when it happened exactly. Somewhere in the years when [[Campaign Knowledge/Characters/Aeralon Ashdelve|Aeralon]]'s change had become impossible to explain away, when the reclusiveness had settled into something harder and the [[Campaign Knowledge/Locations/Grainfall]] obsession had moved from background noise to the only frequency his father seemed to be receiving. Brenvaal came. There was a conversation behind a closed door. Zeph caught the register of it, the particular quality of voices that are being kept down but are not entirely succeeding, the kind of conversation that has weight to it and knows it. He could not hear the words.

He does not know what Brenvaal knew. He knows that whatever it was, it was enough to bring him to that door and enough to make the conversation behind it the kind that does not stay entirely quiet. He knows that [[Campaign Knowledge/Characters/Aeralon Ashdelve|Aeralon]] listened, because Aeralon always listened to Brenvaal in a way he did not always listen to other people, and that listening did not change the outcome. Whatever decision had been made was already made.

Brenvaal left after that conversation. Zeph does not know if they parted well or badly or somewhere in between. He was not there for the goodbye. He remembers the conversation behind the door and then he remembers Brenvaal being gone and the house being quieter in a way that was different from before.

He has not heard from him since.

## What He Feels

Fondness. That is the word and it is accurate as far as it goes.

There is something underneath the fondness that is harder to name. A sense that Brenvaal represented a version of that period of his life that has since been lost, not just because Brenvaal left but because the household he left was not the household it became. He was there during the years when [[Campaign Knowledge/Characters/Aeralon Ashdelve|Aeralon]] was learning to be a father and doing it with effort and intention and help. He was part of the scaffolding of that time. When the scaffolding came down the building stood on its own, which is what scaffolding is for, but the absence of it changed the texture of things.

[[Campaign Knowledge/Characters/Zephyrix Skydelver|Zeph]] does not think about Brenvaal often. This is not indifference. It is the natural result of time and distance and the fact that he has had many other things to think about. But when he does think about him the thinking is warm, and the memory of the pottery studio and the dig sites and the long evenings of collaborative adult attention is one of the genuinely uncomplicated things in a childhood that became complicated enough.

He is becoming a feeling more than a person. Zeph notices this and finds it faintly sad the way you find things faintly sad when you are too busy surviving to give them the grief they might deserve.

## What He Does Not Know

He does not know what Brenvaal knew about [[Campaign Knowledge/Locations/Grainfall]]. Whatever was said behind that closed door is still behind it.

He does not know where Brenvaal is now or what his work has become or whether the things he learned from [[Campaign Knowledge/Characters/Aeralon Ashdelve|Aeralon]] have led him somewhere interesting or somewhere dangerous or somewhere in between. He does not know if Brenvaal has heard about Aeralon's death. He does not know if Brenvaal knows that Aeralon's son is in [[Campaign Knowledge/Locations/Grainfall]].

He does not know if Brenvaal would tell him what he knew if asked. He suspects yes. Brenvaal was always the kind of person who told you what you needed to know when you needed to know it. But suspicion is not certainty and [[Campaign Knowledge/Characters/Zephyrix Skydelver|Zeph]] has been surprised before by what people chose to keep.

He does not know if he will see him again. He thinks about this occasionally, not with urgency but with the low-level wondering that attaches itself to people from earlier chapters of your life. Whether the story is finished or whether there is more of it left.

He hopes there is more of it. He has questions only Brenvaal can answer. And underneath the questions, simpler than all of them, he would not mind sitting in the same room as someone who knew his father before the change and his mother through his father's eyes and him when he was small enough to fall asleep to the sound of a pottery wheel.

That would be something. That would be enough for now.
