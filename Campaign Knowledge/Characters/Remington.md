# Remington

## Who He Knows Them As

A short figure with a mullet, a yellow bandana, and a paperboy hat. [[Campaign Knowledge/Characters/Zephyrix Skydelver|Zeph]] saw him through the bars of the adjacent cell in [[Campaign Knowledge/Locations/Grainfall]], hitting the bars with a stick and begging the guard to come closer. He had a broken lip and a white button-up shirt stained with blood. On the back of his vest, a heart wrapped in tentacles. On his forearms, heavy wrappings ending in gloves with tentacles creeping up from the edges.

He was arrested for a bar fight. Not because he started it. Because he heard one happening and joined in for the fun of it.

---

## What He Knows

Remington gravitates toward trouble the way some people gravitate toward warmth. He said as much without shame. Where things are happening, he wants to be happening too. This is either a useful quality in a companion or a dangerous one. Possibly both.

He knows [[Campaign Knowledge/Characters/Melina]] by sight and identified her as one of the witches of the coven, the most presentable among them.

---

## What He Does Not Know

Why the tentacle imagery. What his actual skills are beyond enthusiasm for bar fights. Where he came from or what brought him to [[Campaign Knowledge/Locations/Grainfall]]. Whether his eagerness to be in the middle of things extends to the kind of trouble Zeph is carrying.
