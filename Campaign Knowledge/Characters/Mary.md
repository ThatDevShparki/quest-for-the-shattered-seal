# Mary

## Who He Knows Them As

[[Campaign Knowledge/Characters/Ezekiel|Zeke's]] little sister. An orc, a solid six feet tall, which is less than little. Formal dress. Apron on, hair in a bun. She had clearly been working all day. A very friendly and approachable smile.

She arrived at the jail with [[Campaign Knowledge/Characters/Melina]] to bail the party out on behalf of [[Campaign Knowledge/Characters/Miss DuBois]].

---

## What He Knows

She works in [[Campaign Knowledge/Locations/Grainfall]], likely at the Sugar Back Eatery on the first floor of [[Campaign Knowledge/Locations/The See Inn]]. She and Zeke have the particular dynamic of siblings who turned out differently and know it. He tensed when she walked in.

---

## What He Does Not Know

Almost everything. Why she works for or with DuBois. What she knows about the local situation. Whether her connection to the party extends beyond this single favor.
