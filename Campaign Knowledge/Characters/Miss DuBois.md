# Miss DuBois

## Who He Knows Them As

She lay in bed at [[Campaign Knowledge/Locations/The See Inn]] on the second floor, skin green and faint with sickly green lines running through it. Pale white. Green beady eyes. One leg gone, replaced by a prosthetic. She bailed the entire party out of jail for fifty gold through [[Campaign Knowledge/Characters/Mary]] and [[Campaign Knowledge/Characters/Melina]], then requested an audience.

[[Campaign Knowledge/Characters/Zephyrix Skydelver|Zeph]] recognized the name before he met her. She was from [[Campaign Knowledge/Organizations/The Orryn Society]]. An adventurer, a fighter, a front woman for the Society in its dealings. He did not know this from [[Campaign Knowledge/Items/Aeralon's Journal|his father's journal]]. He knew it from being in the area, from the specific kind of local knowledge you absorb when you pay attention.

She is the first living person he has met who knew [[Campaign Knowledge/Characters/Aeralon Ashdelve|his father]] professionally.

---

## What He Knows

Miss DuBois laid out a map of the island with three locations marked: Cibola, the Drowned Library, and a campsite about ten miles from town. The campsite is where she last saw [[Campaign Knowledge/Characters/Aeralon Ashdelve|Aeralon]].

She said he had found something in the jungle and needed help navigating [[Campaign Knowledge/Items/Aeralon's Journal|the journal]]. He seemed crazed. There was an attack from monsters at the camp. She lost her leg. She got out alive but does not know how. She does not know how Aeralon made it out either.

She told them Aeralon bailed her out of jail when she was young. She was returning the favor.

She gave them two hundred gold and the quest: go to the campsite, see what is in the jungle.

---

## What He Does Not Know

What she knew about [[Campaign Knowledge/Characters/Aeralon Ashdelve|Aeralon's]] research specifically. What "crazed" looked like, whether it was the Dreamrot or something else. What the monsters at the campsite were. How she lost her leg and what attacked them. What her relationship to [[Campaign Knowledge/Organizations/The Orryn Society|the Orryn Society]] is now, whether she is still connected to them or has been cut loose.

What the sickness is that has turned her skin green with sickly lines. Whether it is related to what she encountered at the campsite. Whether it is related to what consumed Aeralon.

Why she chose to bail out a group of strangers and send them toward the thing that nearly killed her.

## What He Feels

Cautious trust. She knew his father. She bailed them out. She gave them gold and a destination and a map. But she is clearly dying of something, and her connection to [[Campaign Knowledge/Organizations/The Orryn Society]] raises as many questions as it answers. [[Campaign Knowledge/Characters/Aeralon Ashdelve|Aeralon]] withdrew from the Society during his descent. Whatever pushed him away from them, DuBois was still connected to.

She is a source of answers. She may also be a warning of what those answers cost.
