# Malcarion

## Who He Knows Them As

A tall man covered in scars from a lifetime of fighting. [[Campaign Knowledge/Characters/Zephyrix Skydelver|Zeph]] met him in the jail cell in [[Campaign Knowledge/Locations/Grainfall]] on the morning of Session 1. He was wearing what amounted to a potato sack, bruised, staring off into the dirt. Arrested for a bar fight.

When the older man began coughing blood, Malcarion was the one who picked him up and carried him to the wall so [[Campaign Knowledge/Characters/Calis|Calis]] could heal him. He did this without hesitation, which is the first thing Zeph noticed about him beyond the scars.

---

## What He Knows

Malcarion is apparently famous in something called the blood sport. He carries a trident, a short bow, and a whip at his collar. His armor is a patchwork of leather, greaves, and practical fighting gear topped by a large bug-eyed helmet with a crest. He looks like someone who has spent his entire life learning how to hurt things and be hurt by them in return.

He mentioned that the Diamond Trading Company brings in monsters from the mainland, and that someone uses syringes on them. He did not elaborate on whether the syringe user was making the monsters or just controlling them. The distinction matters.

---

## What He Does Not Know

Almost everything. He does not know what Malcarion fights for, what brought him to [[Campaign Knowledge/Locations/Grainfall]], or whether the blood sport he is famous in is sanctioned or underground. He does not know why a man built for combat was sitting quietly in a jail cell over a bar fight rather than something worse.
