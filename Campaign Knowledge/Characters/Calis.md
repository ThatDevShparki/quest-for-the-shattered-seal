# Calis

## Who He Knows Them As

Antlers. Prim and proper clothes like a uniform. An amulet necklace with a picture of a waterfall on it. She had been watching the scene in the cell with the quiet observance of someone who catalogues what she sees. She did not look like she belonged in jail. That impression turned out to be correct. She had thought she was helping with an investigation and was actually trespassing.

[[Campaign Knowledge/Characters/Zephyrix Skydelver|Zeph]] met her in the jail cell in [[Campaign Knowledge/Locations/Grainfall]] on the morning of Session 1.

---

## What He Knows

Calis can heal. When [[Campaign Knowledge/Characters/Malcarion|Malcarion]] carried [[Campaign Knowledge/Characters/Ra]] to the wall, Calis cast Lay on Hands while drinking a vial. She also used Prestidigitation to clean everyone afterward, which was practical and strangely generous in a jail cell at dawn.

She licked the blood off her hands afterward. Zeph noticed this. When they spoke later, walking through the festival, she explained. Where she was taught, they use blood to learn more about people. Technically by people taught very far away. Not a vampire. She called it learning. The explanation was matter-of-fact and did not invite further questions.

She recognized the dead child from the pig attack as one of her students. She said the child hung around the edges of town near the farms. She knew this child. That changes the quality of what happened from an abstract horror to a personal one.

---

## What He Does Not Know

What Calis is, precisely. The antlers, the blood reading, the healing, the uniform. She does not fit a category he has encountered before. He does not know where she was taught or by whom or what the learning through blood actually reveals. He does not know why she was investigating in [[Campaign Knowledge/Locations/Grainfall]] or what she thought the investigation was about before the trespassing charge.

## What He Feels

Careful. Precise. She observes before she acts and acts without asking permission. The blood licking should have been unsettling. It was not. The way she explained it carried the weight of someone who has had to explain it before and has stopped caring whether the explanation is accepted.

She knew the dead child. That is the detail Zeph will not forget about her.
