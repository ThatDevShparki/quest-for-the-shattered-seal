# Ra

## Who He Knows Them As

An older man. White hair to his shoulders. Deeply tanned skin, sunburnt. A goatee with a long curly bit, clean-shaven on the sides. One gold loop in a single ear. He was wearing blue clothing with gold and silver detailing, finer than what a man in a jail cell should be wearing.

He was in the cell with [[Campaign Knowledge/Characters/Zephyrix Skydelver|Zeph]] and [[Campaign Knowledge/Characters/Malcarion|Malcarion]] in [[Campaign Knowledge/Locations/Grainfall]] on the morning of Session 1. He began coughing blood. [[Campaign Knowledge/Characters/Calis]] healed him. He woke enough to speak, and what they heard was "Ra." He said to call him Ra. Something was odd about the way he spoke, as though what they heard was not exactly what he said.

Then he left. [[Campaign Knowledge/Characters/Ezekiel|Zeke]] chased after him. When Zeke came back, he said the man told him his name was [[Campaign Knowledge/Characters/The Architect]].

---

## What He Knows

Almost nothing concrete. The man was sick. He was healed. He left quickly. He gave two names, one to the group and one to Zeke. Whether "Ra" and "the Architect" are different names for the same identity, a title and a name, or something else entirely, Zeph cannot say.

What Zeph knows is that the name hit him like a wall.

The Architect. A warm smile and circular glasses at his mother's bedside the night before she died. A name that was not a name, given to a child who asked because he asked the name of every guest. [[Campaign Knowledge/Characters/Zephyrix Skydelver|Zeph]] has carried that memory for eighteen years without it meaning anything beyond a strange detail in the story of his mother's death. He never connected it to malice or mystery. It was a warm memory. A kind man with a nice smile who came to help and left before morning.

Now the same name comes out of the mouth of a bleeding man in a jail cell in [[Campaign Knowledge/Locations/Grainfall]], and everything Zeph thought he knew about that memory shifts. Not into clarity. Into confusion. Was the visitor at [[Campaign Knowledge/Characters/Sylara Dawnmere|Sylara's]] bedside the same person as this man? Is "the Architect" a title rather than a name, something that can be passed between people? Did the man at his mother's bedside help her or do something else entirely?

He has never questioned his mother's death before. The illness came and the physicians failed and she died. That was the shape of it. Now that shape has a crack in it.

---

## What He Does Not Know

Whether Ra is the Architect from his mother's bedside. Whether "the Architect" is a person or a role. Why Ra was in the jail cell. Why he was coughing blood. Why his speech did not match what they heard. Where he went when he left. Whether the syringes [[Campaign Knowledge/Characters/Ezekiel|Zeke]] mentioned in connection with the Architect are connected to the injected pigs at the festival. Whether this man knew [[Campaign Knowledge/Characters/Aeralon Ashdelve|Aeralon]].

Whether his mother's death was what it appeared to be.

## What He Feels

The name landed before the thinking could start. The Architect. The sound of it in [[Campaign Knowledge/Characters/Ezekiel|Zeke's]] mouth and the way it reached into the earliest part of his memory and pulled. Not fear. Not anger. Confusion. The specific disorientation of a memory that has been filed as complete and is suddenly, violently, not.

He has never questioned his mother's death. He is questioning it now.
