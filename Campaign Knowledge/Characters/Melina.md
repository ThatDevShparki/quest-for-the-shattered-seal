# Melina

## Who He Knows Them As

A witchy-looking young woman. Large-brimmed hat, the almond shape common in [[Campaign Knowledge/Locations/Grainfall]]. A veil over a long black coat. Through the veil, tattoos on a young face.

She arrived at the jail with [[Campaign Knowledge/Characters/Mary]] to bail the party out on behalf of [[Campaign Knowledge/Characters/Miss DuBois]].

---

## What He Knows

[[Campaign Knowledge/Characters/Remington]] identified her immediately as one of the witches of the coven, the most presentable among them. The coven backs the Dixie clan. Beyond this, Zeph knows what a history check told him: the witches are a known presence in [[Campaign Knowledge/Locations/Grainfall]]. Melina is the one they send when they want to make a good impression.

---

## What He Does Not Know

What the coven does, what the Dixie clan is, why a witch was sent to bail out strangers, and what connection exists between the coven and [[Campaign Knowledge/Characters/Miss DuBois]].
