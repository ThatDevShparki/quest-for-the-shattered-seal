# Ezekiel

## Who He Knows Them As

Seven feet tall. Green skin. Tattooed. A dress shirt and gold chains and suspenders that suggested someone who takes care of how he looks and does not care if you know it. [[Campaign Knowledge/Characters/Zephyrix Skydelver|Zeph]] saw him in the adjacent cell in [[Campaign Knowledge/Locations/Grainfall]].

Arrested for a card game gone wrong. He had bought himself in with moonshine, people did not appreciate the cheating, a fight broke out, he cast some magic, there was an explosion. He told this story without remorse.

---

## What He Knows

Zeke is an orc with some kind of arcane ability. He used Mage Hand to steal the cell keys from the guard, which was both helpful and concerning. He tensed visibly when [[Campaign Knowledge/Characters/Mary]] arrived, calling her "another bleeding heart." She is his little sister. The dynamic between them carries the specific texture of a sibling relationship where one of them turned out respectable and the other did not.

When [[Campaign Knowledge/Characters/Ra]] left the cell, Zeke was the one who chased after him. He came back and told the group the old man said his name was the Architect. Zeke also mentioned that the Architect uses syringes on monsters. He said [[Campaign Knowledge/Characters/Malcarion|Mal]] brings the monsters in and the Architect injects them.

---

## What He Does Not Know

What Zeke's actual capabilities are beyond explosions and Mage Hand. Where the gold chains come from. What his relationship with [[Campaign Knowledge/Locations/Grainfall]] is beyond having a sister who works here. How much he knows about the Architect beyond the syringes.

## What He Feels

Loud. Direct. The kind of person who takes up exactly as much space as he occupies and does not apologize for any of it. Not someone Zeph would have chosen as a companion. But he was the one who followed Ra. That matters.
