# Zephyrix Skydelver

![[Resources/Images/Zephyrix Skydelver - Image.png]]

## Who He Is

Aarakocra. Cleric of [[Campaign Knowledge/Organizations/The Orryn Society|Oghma]], Knowledge Domain. Level 3. Twenty three years old. Tawny gold and cream plumage with a dark slate blue feather crest that refuses to behave. Round wire spectacles, slightly askew. Amber eyes. Lean and slightly short for his peers. 4'9", 85 lbs.

The name is a union. [[Campaign Knowledge/Characters/Sylara Dawnmere|Sylara's]] sky, [[Campaign Knowledge/Characters/Aeralon Ashdelve|Aeralon's]] delving. A triumph of a word.

---

## His Story

He was born into love and raised into loss. [[Campaign Knowledge/Characters/Sylara Dawnmere|His mother]] died when he was not yet five. [[Campaign Knowledge/Characters/The Architect|A man with a warm smile and circular glasses]] came to her bedside the night before and gave a title instead of a name. [[Campaign Knowledge/Characters/Aeralon Ashdelve|His father]] grieved badly and then with extraordinary intention, and decided his son would come with him to every dig, every archive, every ruin. Side by side, patiently, with genuine delight. [[Campaign Knowledge/Characters/Brenvaal]], an Aarakocra artisan and Aeralon's understudy, helped hold the household together in those early years. The full account of how it began is told in [[Campaign Notes/Story/01 - The Origin|The Origin]].

Then [[Campaign Knowledge/Characters/Aeralon Ashdelve|Aeralon]] changed. Slowly and then completely. He made what he called his ultimate discovery in [[Campaign Knowledge/Locations/Grainfall]] and it consumed him. He pushed his son away. He grew reclusive, bitter, angry in ways that had no target. He died without explanation, without reconciliation. He left only [[Campaign Knowledge/Items/Aeralon's Journal|the journal]]. Inside it, Zeph found his mother's handwriting on the first page, instructions to look up and show their son the sky, and understood everything about his father's life as a father and nothing about what had destroyed him. He took [[Campaign Knowledge/Items/Sylara's Amulet|his mother's amulet]] from the wooden box and flew toward [[Campaign Knowledge/Locations/Grainfall|Grainfall]]. The full account is told in [[Campaign Notes/Story/02 - The Descent|The Descent]].

In [[Campaign Knowledge/Locations/Grainfall]] he found [[Campaign Knowledge/Locations/The Ruined Observatory]] at the forest's edge, a tapestry bearing [[Campaign Knowledge/Organizations/The Dawnmere Lineage|the Dawnmere crest]] and [[Campaign Knowledge/Clues/The Four Symbols|four variations of a shared base form]], and someone living on the upper levels. He entered [[Campaign Knowledge/Locations/The Sealed Ruin Beneath Grainfall|the sealed ruin beneath the town]] and found a civilization that history had swallowed whole. In the ceremonial room he found his mother's family crest woven into a royal bloodline carved in stone that predated recorded history. The full account is told in [[Campaign Notes/Story/03 - Arrival in Grainfall|Arrival in Grainfall]].

He performed the ritual. [[Campaign Knowledge/Characters/The Visage|Something spoke back]] in [[Campaign Knowledge/Characters/Sylara Dawnmere|his mother's]] voice without her warmth and asked him to become its servant. He ran. The ruin erupted behind him. The authorities found nothing disturbed. He was arrested. [[Campaign Knowledge/Items/Sylara's Amulet|The amulet]] grew warm against his chest for the first time. The full account is told in [[Campaign Notes/Story/04 - The Ritual|The Ritual]].

In the cell, [[Campaign Knowledge/Characters/Ari|a raven]] pulled itself from [[Campaign Knowledge/Items/Aeralon's Journal|the journal]] like smoke finding a shape. He named it [[Campaign Knowledge/Characters/Ari]], short for [[Campaign Knowledge/Characters/Aeralon Ashdelve|Aeralon]]. It speaks in his father's cadences. During erratic spurts it says _never-dark_ and _Dawnmere-burns_. He does not know yet what it is trying to say. The full account is told in [[Campaign Notes/Story/05 - The Cell|The Cell]].

He is not proud of running. His mother would not have run. He is going back. And this time he is going to answer.

## Bonds, Ideals, Flaws

**Bond:** The dying grace of both his parents. [[Campaign Knowledge/Characters/Sylara Dawnmere|His mother's]] final words, [[Campaign Knowledge/Characters/Aeralon Ashdelve|his father's]] final warning. He carries both as a sacred charge. To honor them is to decide, not react.

**Ideals:**

- Patience. I will not be moved by fear or hunger. I stand still, I decide, and I move on my own terms.
- Knowledge is sacred, not absolute. Understanding something is only meaningful if you remain yourself long enough to use it.
- The past serves the living. We learn from what came before so we can be present for what stands in front of us now.

**Flaws:**

- Patience can become paralysis. Not every moment allows for deliberation.
- He is his father's son. The thrill of discovery pulls at him. He knows where obsession leads and is not immune to it.
- He is still young. Clumsy, curious, occasionally oblivious. He will follow an interesting thing further than is wise and trip over his own feet doing it.

## The Core Question

> "What would your character do if they discovered something that could change the world, but at a cost to themselves?"

He would stand still. He would not be moved by fear or by hunger. He would wait until he understood what it actually cost and who it would actually cost and whether the world it would change was one worth changing at that price. And then he would decide. Deliberately. As himself. Not as his father's obsession and not as his mother's sacrifice. As Zephyrix Skydelver.

## Allies

**[[Campaign Knowledge/Characters/Brenvaal]]**

An Aarakocra artisan, potter and smith, who studied the ancient world under [[Campaign Knowledge/Characters/Aeralon Ashdelve]] not as a scholar but as a craftsman seeking to understand what the old civilizations knew that the modern world had forgotten. He learned enough. Then he left to hone what he had learned in his own workshop, on his own terms.

He was present in the years immediately after [[Campaign Knowledge/Characters/Sylara Dawnmere|Sylara]] died, helping hold the household together while [[Campaign Knowledge/Characters/Aeralon Ashdelve|Aeralon]] found his footing as a father. Zeph was young. He remembers [[Campaign Knowledge/Characters/Brenvaal]] more as a feeling than a person. Steadiness in an unsteady time. Hands that were always doing something useful.

The last anyone heard, he and [[Campaign Knowledge/Characters/Aeralon Ashdelve|Aeralon]] had a quiet but tense conversation about [[Campaign Knowledge/Locations/Grainfall]]. [[Campaign Knowledge/Characters/Brenvaal]] knew enough about what Aeralon had found there to argue against going further. Aeralon listened, considered, and went anyway. They did not part badly. They parted as two people who respected each other enough to disagree without breaking.

Brenvaal does not know Zeph is in [[Campaign Knowledge/Locations/Grainfall]]. Not yet. But he is the kind of person who shows up when it matters, which is exactly why a grieving wizard trusted him with his son twenty years ago.

## Organizations

**[[Campaign Knowledge/Organizations/The Orryn Society]]**

One of the foremost archaeological and historical institutions in the known world. [[Campaign Knowledge/Characters/Aeralon Ashdelve]] was not merely a member. He was a pioneer, a celebrated voice, someone whose name opened doors and whose publications shaped how [[Campaign Knowledge/Organizations/The Orryn Society|the Society]] understood the ancient world. He gave the institution some of its most significant discoveries and most of its best arguments.

Zeph grew up in that orbit. He knew the faces, the names, the particular smell of the Society's archives. He attended gatherings as a child without fully understanding what they were. These are feelings more than memories. Warmth and candlelight and adults talking about things he was only beginning to understand.

He has no formal standing with [[Campaign Knowledge/Organizations/The Orryn Society|the Society]]. He was never inducted, never employed, never officially anything. He is simply [[Campaign Knowledge/Characters/Aeralon Ashdelve|Aeralon's]] son, which in certain rooms means everything and in others means nothing at all. How the Society regards Aeralon's final years, his reclusiveness, his apparent deterioration, and now his son's arrest in [[Campaign Knowledge/Locations/Grainfall]], is unknown.

**[[Campaign Knowledge/Organizations/The Dawnmere Lineage]]**

Not an institution. Not a faction. A bloodline old enough to appear carved into the walls of [[Campaign Knowledge/Locations/The Sealed Ruin Beneath Grainfall|a sealed ruin]] that predates recorded history. [[Campaign Knowledge/Characters/Sylara Dawnmere]] knew what she came from and chose differently. She never spoke of it. She wore the mark of her bloodline as a holy symbol and made it mean something other than what it had been given to her to mean.

Zeph knows the crest. He knows his mother wore it and never explained it. He knows what he saw on that wall in [[Campaign Knowledge/Locations/Grainfall]], and he knows what his father drew over and over in the margins of [[Campaign Knowledge/Items/Aeralon's Journal|a journal]] before destroying every trace of it. He knows that [[Campaign Knowledge/Clues/The Four Symbols|the four symbols]] at [[Campaign Knowledge/Locations/The Ruined Observatory]] are variations of the same base form, and that his family crest is one of them.

Whether living members of [[Campaign Knowledge/Organizations/The Dawnmere Lineage|the Dawnmere line]] exist, whether they are aware of him, whether [[Campaign Knowledge/Characters/The Visage|the voice]] that spoke to him in [[Campaign Knowledge/Locations/The Sealed Ruin Beneath Grainfall|that ceremonial room]] has servants in the world who share his blood, is entirely unknown.

He is intimately tied to both of these organizations whether he claims them or not. He did not choose either. He was born into one and raised in the shadow of the other. Finding his footing means eventually deciding what he owes them, if anything, and what they owe him.

## Enemies

**[[Campaign Knowledge/Characters/The Architect]]**

A figure from the night [[Campaign Knowledge/Characters/Sylara Dawnmere]] died. Zeph was not yet five years old. He learned of him not from memory but from [[Campaign Knowledge/Characters/Aeralon Ashdelve|Aeralon]], who described the visitor matter of factly over the years whenever Zeph asked about his mother's death. A man with a warm smile and circular glasses who came to her bedside, gave his name as the Architect when a curious child asked, and disappeared before morning.

Zeph does not know what he is. Not a physician. Not a cleric. Something that arrived with a title instead of a name and left before anyone could ask further questions. Several physicians and clerics had come to [[Campaign Knowledge/Characters/Sylara Dawnmere|Sylara's]] aid in the days prior. Nothing had worked. Whatever the Architect came to do, he was the last to try and she was gone by morning.

For most of his life this was simply a strange detail in the story of his mother's death. One of those things that sits in the back of your mind without context, neither urgent nor forgotten.

It sits differently now. Zeph cannot say exactly why. Nothing he has encountered in [[Campaign Knowledge/Locations/Grainfall]] has given him specific reason to connect [[Campaign Knowledge/Characters/The Architect]] to any of it. [[Campaign Knowledge/Characters/The Visage|The voice]] in [[Campaign Knowledge/Locations/The Sealed Ruin Beneath Grainfall|the ceremonial room]] and the man at his mother's bedside live in separate places in his mind. But the story that used to feel strange now feels deliberate. A warm smile aimed at a child who would grow up to ask questions. A name chosen to be remembered rather than forgotten.

He is not an enemy Zeph has named as such. He is something Zeph has become wary of without fully understanding why. The difference matters. Named enemies can be confronted. This is something else. A shape at the edge of what he knows, patient and unhurried, that he is not yet sure is looking back.
