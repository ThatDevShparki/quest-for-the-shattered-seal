# Zephyrix Skydelver

![[Resources/Images/Zephyrix Skydelver - Image.png]]

## Who He Is

Aarakocra. Cleric of [[The Orryn Society|Oghma]], Knowledge Domain. Level 3. Twenty three years old. Tawny gold and cream plumage with a dark slate blue feather crest that refuses to behave. Round wire spectacles, slightly askew. Amber eyes. Lean and slightly short for his peers. 4'9", 85 lbs.

The name is a union. [[Sylara Dawnmere|Sylara's]] sky, [[Aeralon Ashdelve|Aeralon's]] delving. A triumph of a word.

---

## His Story

He was born into love and raised into loss. [[Sylara Dawnmere|His mother]] died when he was not yet five. [[The Architect|A man with a warm smile and circular glasses]] came to her bedside the night before and gave a title instead of a name. [[Aeralon Ashdelve|His father]] grieved badly and then with extraordinary intention, and decided his son would come with him to every dig, every archive, every ruin. Side by side, patiently, with genuine delight. [[Brenvaal]], an Aarakocra artisan and [[Aeralon Ashdelve|Aeralon's]] understudy, helped hold the household together in those early years. The full account of how it began is told in [[Campaign Notes/Story/01 - The Origin|The Origin]].

Then [[Aeralon Ashdelve|Aeralon]] changed. Slowly and then completely. He made what he called his ultimate discovery in [[Grainfall]] and it consumed him. He pushed his son away. He grew reclusive, bitter, angry in ways that had no target. He died without explanation, without reconciliation. He left only [[Aeralon's Journal|the journal]]. Inside it, [[Zephyrix Skydelver|Zeph]] found his mother's handwriting on the first page, instructions to look up and show their son the sky, and understood everything about his father's life as a father and nothing about what had destroyed him. He took [[Sylara's Amulet|his mother's amulet]] from the wooden box and flew toward [[Grainfall]]. The full account is told in [[Campaign Notes/Story/02 - The Descent|The Descent]].

In [[Grainfall]] he found [[The Ruined Observatory]] at the forest's edge, a tapestry bearing [[The Dawnmere Lineage|the Dawnmere crest]] and [[The Four Symbols|four variations of a shared base form]], and someone living on the upper levels. He entered [[The Sealed Ruin Beneath Grainfall|the sealed ruin beneath the town]] and found a civilization that history had swallowed whole. In the ceremonial room he found his mother's family crest woven into a royal bloodline carved in stone that predated recorded history. The full account is told in [[Campaign Notes/Story/03 - Arrival in Grainfall|Arrival in Grainfall]].

He performed the ritual. [[The Visage|Something spoke back]] in [[Sylara Dawnmere|his mother's]] voice without her warmth and asked him to become its servant. He ran. The ruin erupted behind him. The authorities found nothing disturbed. He was arrested. [[Sylara's Amulet|The amulet]] grew warm against his chest for the first time. The full account is told in [[Campaign Notes/Story/04 - The Ritual|The Ritual]].

In the cell, [[Ari|a raven]] pulled itself from [[Aeralon's Journal|the journal]] like smoke finding a shape. He named it [[Ari]], short for [[Aeralon Ashdelve|Aeralon]]. It speaks in his father's cadences. During erratic spurts it says _never-dark_ and _Dawnmere-burns_. He does not know yet what it is trying to say. The full account is told in [[Campaign Notes/Story/05 - The Cell|The Cell]].

He is not proud of running. His mother would not have run. He is going back. And this time he is going to answer.

## Bonds, Ideals, Flaws

**Bond:** The dying grace of both his parents. [[Sylara Dawnmere|His mother's]] final words, [[Aeralon Ashdelve|his father's]] final warning. He carries both as a sacred charge. To honor them is to decide, not react.

**Ideals:**

- Patience. I will not be moved by fear or hunger. I stand still, I decide, and I move on my own terms.
- Knowledge is sacred, not absolute. Understanding something is only meaningful if you remain yourself long enough to use it.
- The past serves the living. We learn from what came before so we can be present for what stands in front of us now.

**Flaws:**

- Patience can become paralysis. Not every moment allows for deliberation.
- He is his father's son. The thrill of discovery pulls at him. He knows where obsession leads and is not immune to it.
- He is still young. Clumsy, curious, occasionally oblivious. He will follow an interesting thing further than is wise and trip over his own feet doing it.

## The Core Question

> "What would your character do if they discovered something that could change the world, but at a cost to themselves?"

He would stand still. He would not be moved by fear or by hunger. He would wait until he understood what it actually cost and who it would actually cost and whether the world it would change was one worth changing at that price. And then he would decide. Deliberately. As himself. Not as his father's obsession and not as his mother's sacrifice. As Zephyrix Skydelver.

## Allies

**[[Brenvaal]]**

An Aarakocra artisan, potter and smith, who studied the ancient world under [[Aeralon Ashdelve]] not as a scholar but as a craftsman seeking to understand what the old civilizations knew that the modern world had forgotten. He learned enough. Then he left to hone what he had learned in his own workshop, on his own terms.

He was present in the years immediately after [[Sylara Dawnmere|Sylara]] died, helping hold the household together while [[Aeralon Ashdelve|Aeralon]] found his footing as a father. [[Zephyrix Skydelver|Zeph]] was young. He remembers [[Brenvaal]] more as a feeling than a person. Steadiness in an unsteady time. Hands that were always doing something useful.

The last anyone heard, he and [[Aeralon Ashdelve|Aeralon]] had a quiet but tense conversation about [[Grainfall]]. [[Brenvaal]] knew enough about what [[Aeralon Ashdelve|Aeralon]] had found there to argue against going further. [[Aeralon Ashdelve|Aeralon]] listened, considered, and went anyway. They did not part badly. They parted as two people who respected each other enough to disagree without breaking.

[[Brenvaal]] does not know [[Zephyrix Skydelver|Zeph]] is in [[Grainfall]]. Not yet. But he is the kind of person who shows up when it matters, which is exactly why a grieving wizard trusted him with his son twenty years ago.

## Organizations

**[[The Orryn Society]]**

One of the foremost archaeological and historical institutions in the known world. [[Aeralon Ashdelve]] was not merely a member. He was a pioneer, a celebrated voice, someone whose name opened doors and whose publications shaped how [[The Orryn Society|the Society]] understood the ancient world. He gave the institution some of its most significant discoveries and most of its best arguments.

[[Zephyrix Skydelver|Zeph]] grew up in that orbit. He knew the faces, the names, the particular smell of [[The Orryn Society|the Society's]] archives. He attended gatherings as a child without fully understanding what they were. These are feelings more than memories. Warmth and candlelight and adults talking about things he was only beginning to understand.

He has no formal standing with [[The Orryn Society|the Society]]. He was never inducted, never employed, never officially anything. He is simply [[Aeralon Ashdelve|Aeralon's]] son, which in certain rooms means everything and in others means nothing at all. How [[The Orryn Society|the Society]] regards [[Aeralon Ashdelve|Aeralon's]] final years, his reclusiveness, his apparent deterioration, and now his son's arrest in [[Grainfall]], is unknown.

**[[The Dawnmere Lineage]]**

Not an institution. Not a faction. A bloodline old enough to appear carved into the walls of [[The Sealed Ruin Beneath Grainfall|a sealed ruin]] that predates recorded history. [[Sylara Dawnmere]] knew what she came from and chose differently. She never spoke of it. She wore the mark of her bloodline as a holy symbol and made it mean something other than what it had been given to her to mean.

[[Zephyrix Skydelver|Zeph]] knows the crest. He knows his mother wore it and never explained it. He knows what he saw on that wall in [[Grainfall]], and he knows what his father drew over and over in the margins of [[Aeralon's Journal|a journal]] before destroying every trace of it. He knows that [[The Four Symbols|the four symbols]] at [[The Ruined Observatory]] are variations of the same base form, and that his family crest is one of them.

Whether living members of [[The Dawnmere Lineage|the Dawnmere line]] exist, whether they are aware of him, whether [[The Visage|the voice]] that spoke to him in [[The Sealed Ruin Beneath Grainfall|that ceremonial room]] has servants in the world who share his blood, is entirely unknown.

He is intimately tied to both of these organizations whether he claims them or not. He did not choose either. He was born into one and raised in the shadow of the other. Finding his footing means eventually deciding what he owes them, if anything, and what they owe him.

## Enemies

**[[The Architect]]**

A figure from the night [[Sylara Dawnmere]] died. [[Zephyrix Skydelver|Zeph]] was not yet five years old. He learned of him not from memory but from [[Aeralon Ashdelve|Aeralon]], who described the visitor matter of factly over the years whenever [[Zephyrix Skydelver|Zeph]] asked about his mother's death. A man with a warm smile and circular glasses who came to her bedside, gave his name as [[The Architect]] when a curious child asked, and disappeared before morning.

[[Zephyrix Skydelver|Zeph]] does not know what he is. Not a physician. Not a cleric. Something that arrived with a title instead of a name and left before anyone could ask further questions. Several physicians and clerics had come to [[Sylara Dawnmere|Sylara's]] aid in the days prior. Nothing had worked. Whatever [[The Architect]] came to do, he was the last to try and she was gone by morning.

For most of his life this was simply a strange detail in the story of his mother's death. One of those things that sits in the back of your mind without context, neither urgent nor forgotten.

It sits differently now. [[Zephyrix Skydelver|Zeph]] cannot say exactly why. Nothing he has encountered in [[Grainfall]] has given him specific reason to connect [[The Architect]] to any of it. [[The Visage|The voice]] in [[The Sealed Ruin Beneath Grainfall|the ceremonial room]] and the man at his mother's bedside live in separate places in his mind. But the story that used to feel strange now feels deliberate. A warm smile aimed at a child who would grow up to ask questions. A name chosen to be remembered rather than forgotten.

He is not an enemy [[Zephyrix Skydelver|Zeph]] has named as such. He is something [[Zephyrix Skydelver|Zeph]] has become wary of without fully understanding why. The difference matters. Named enemies can be confronted. This is something else. A shape at the edge of what he knows, patient and unhurried, that he is not yet sure is looking back.
