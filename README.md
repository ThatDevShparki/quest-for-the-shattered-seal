# The Scholar

**Zephyrix "Zeph" Skydelver** is an Aarakocra Cleric of Oghma, Knowledge Domain. Twenty-three years old. Spectacles askew. Feather crest unruly. His mother died before he could know her. His father followed her into something ancient and never came back.

He carries a worn leather journal, a silver pendant bearing a crest that should not exist on the walls of a sealed ruin, and a raven that speaks in a dead man's voice.

He is in a jail cell in Grainfall. Session one begins at dawn.

---

# The Vault

This is not a wiki. It is a player's knowledge base, written entirely from Zeph's perspective. What he knows. What he does not know. What he suspects and what he fears. The gaps are as deliberate as the details.

```
Campaign Knowledge/          Characters, Locations, Items, Clues, Organizations
Campaign Notes/
  Story/                     The canonical narrative, told chapter by chapter
  Sessions/                  Session notes and present state
  Questions/                 Every open question Zeph is carrying
Mystery Dashboard.md         Auto-updating investigation board
Home.md                      The vault's front door
```

Every document is linked. Every mystery is tagged. Every question is tracked. The vault grows as the campaign reveals more.

# Conventions

- Everything is written from Zeph's perspective. Third person. No omniscience.
- No em dashes. Deliberate stylistic choice.
- Gaps are named, not papered over. If he does not know, the document says so.
- Player knowledge and character knowledge are kept separate.
- The Campaign Story is the canonical source of truth for what happened.
- Character, location, and item documents are reference profiles that link to the Story.
- Wiki-links connect every meaningful entity mention.
- Mystery thread tags (`mystery/dawnmere`, `mystery/visage`, etc.) power the dashboard.
