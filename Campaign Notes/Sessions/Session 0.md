# Session 0

## What Happened

The three character-creation questions and their answers establish the events that brought [[Campaign Knowledge/Characters/Zephyrix Skydelver|Zeph]] to [[Campaign Knowledge/Locations/Grainfall]] and into a jail cell with [[Campaign Knowledge/Characters/Ari]] on his knee. The full narrative is told across the Campaign Story chapters.

---

### 1. Why did you go into the ruin?

[[Campaign Knowledge/Characters/Aeralon Ashdelve|Aeralon]]'s journal was dense and meticulous in his earlier years. Toward the end the pages changed. The handwriting fractured, references to old gods and rituals crowded the margins, and a ruin considered mythological by most scholars began to take shape in the notes. Then, in the middle of it all, a single sentence written in perfect clarity:

> _"I found her, my love. After 20 years. Sylara is here with me."_

That was what sent him through the door. The full account of his father's decline and the decision to follow him to [[Campaign Knowledge/Locations/Grainfall]] is told in [[Campaign Notes/Story/01 - The Origin|The Origin]] and [[Campaign Notes/Story/02 - The Descent|The Descent]].

---

### 2. What did you see that disturbed you?

[[Campaign Knowledge/Locations/The Sealed Ruin Beneath Grainfall|The sealed ruin]] was extraordinary. Untouched, ancient, perfectly preserved. He found the ceremonial room deep inside, a formal space with braziers and altars arranged on a central platform. The walls told lineage rather than events. And there, woven into the family tree, was his mother's crest.

He opened [[Campaign Knowledge/Items/Aeralon's Journal|the journal]] and found [[Campaign Knowledge/Characters/Aeralon Ashdelve|Aeralon]] had stood in the same spot. The crest drawn over and over, every one defaced. Surrounding the defaced crests, references to old gods and rituals, and beside passages in an unknown language:

_As far as I can tell this is the language of the brothers. It is called [[Campaign Knowledge/Clues/Sae-th]]. It binds the world._

It was not [[Campaign Knowledge/Organizations/The Dawnmere Lineage|the Dawnmere crest]] on the wall that disturbed him. It was the realization that his father had not been mad. He had been desperately trying to leave a message. The full narrative of the arrival and exploration is told in [[Campaign Notes/Story/03 - Arrival in Grainfall|Arrival in Grainfall]].

---

### 3. What did you do that got you arrested?

He performed the ritual alone in the ceremonial room. Something spoke back. A woman's voice. At first he thought it was his mother. It was not. It was something that knew exactly how to sound like her, something that had found the most unguarded place in him and pressed itself against it like a key against a lock.

[[Campaign Knowledge/Characters/The Visage]] asked him to become its servant. He ran. The room erupted behind him. When the authorities went to look, the ruin was exactly as it had always been. No sign of disturbance. He was arrested for trespassing and for being dangerously and publicly out of his mind.

He knows what he heard. He knows what it felt like. And he knows that he did not say no. He simply ran. The full ritual and flight are told in [[Campaign Notes/Story/04 - The Ritual|The Ritual]].

---

### What he did not notice until the cell

In the dark of the cell, after the guards had gone, [[Campaign Knowledge/Items/Aeralon's Journal|the journal]] moved. A tremor along the cover. Then [[Campaign Knowledge/Characters/Ari]] pulled itself out of the pages like smoke finding a shape. It landed on his knee and looked at him with eyes that carried something he almost recognized. Not his mother. Not the voice from the ritual. Something older and closer and quieter than either.

[[Campaign Knowledge/Characters/Ari]] spoke in [[Campaign Knowledge/Characters/Aeralon Ashdelve|Aeralon]]'s cadences. Fragments. Observations about stonework. Half-finished sentences from field journals [[Campaign Knowledge/Characters/Zephyrix Skydelver|Zeph]] had never been allowed to read. His inheritance was never just [[Campaign Knowledge/Items/Aeralon's Journal|the journal]]. It was what lived inside it. The full account of [[Campaign Knowledge/Characters/Ari]]'s emergence is told in [[Campaign Notes/Story/05 - The Cell|The Cell]].

## What Changed

> _"What would your character do if they discovered something that could change the world — but at a cost to themselves?"_

He would not pursue it and he would not retreat from it.

He has watched what pursuit looks like. His father ran toward the dark until it swallowed him, page by page, year by year, until the man who had closed [[Campaign Knowledge/Items/Aeralon's Journal|a journal]] on every dig to show his son something wonderful became something that pushed his son away and ran toward death in ways that were unlike his character. [[Campaign Knowledge/Characters/Aeralon Ashdelve|Aeralon]] did not choose the cost. He simply kept going until the cost chose him.

He has watched what retreat looks like too. His mother looked at what her bloodline was and what it asked of the people who carried it and chose differently. She built her life in the opposite direction, became a cleric of holy light, wore the mark of her inheritance as a holy symbol and made it mean something else. She paid a different cost. Whether she chose it or whether it was taken from him is a question [[Campaign Knowledge/Characters/Zephyrix Skydelver|Zeph]] does not yet have the answer to. He knows only that a man with a warm smile and circular glasses walked into her room the night before she died, gave his name as [[Campaign Knowledge/Characters/The Architect]] when a curious child asked, and was gone before morning.

Neither of them got to decide on their own terms. That is what he carries. Not their answers. Their cautionary tales.

So his answer is not yes and it is not no. It is not heroic and it is not wise. It is the answer of someone who has inherited two different kinds of loss and is trying to learn from both of them at the same time.

He would stand still. He would not be moved by fear or by hunger. He would look at the thing clearly, the way his mother looked at her own inheritance, without flinching, without reaching. He would wait until he understood what it actually cost and who it would actually cost and whether the world it would change was one worth changing at that price.

And then he would decide. Deliberately. As himself. Not as his father's obsession and not as his mother's sacrifice. As [[Campaign Knowledge/Characters/Zephyrix Skydelver]], who is twenty three years old and freshly alone and has not yet earned all the wisdom he is reaching for but is reaching for it anyway.

He reacted once in that ceremonial room. He ran from a voice that asked him a question he was not ready to answer. He resents that. His mother would not have run. She would have stood in that room and looked at whatever was speaking and made her choice with both eyes open.

He is going back. And this time he is going to answer.

Whatever it costs him, he will have chosen it. That is the only thing he knows for certain. The cost is coming regardless. The only question is whether he meets it on his own terms or lets it collect without his consent the way it collected from everyone else who carried this name.

He will stand still. He will decide. He will not react.

That is all he has. For now it is enough.

## The Shared Rumor Exercise

_Three things people say about the ruins. What [[Campaign Knowledge/Characters/Zephyrix Skydelver|Zeph]] thinks when he hears them._

---

### "The ruins were built to worship something beneath the world."

He has stood in that ceremonial room. He has seen the altars and the braziers and the family tree carved into the walls with his mother's crest woven into the bloodline of something ancient enough to predate recorded history. He has read his father's annotation about [[Campaign Knowledge/Clues/Sae-th]], the language of [[Campaign Knowledge/Clues/The Brothers]], the thing that binds the world.

Worship is the wrong word. He does not say this out loud, not yet, not to people who have not been inside. But he thinks it every time he hears this rumor.

The room was not built for devotion. It was built for something more specific than that. The braziers and altars and the arrangement of the space, he knows ceremonial architecture, his father taught him to read a room the way other fathers teach children to read words, and what that room described was not prayer. It was contract. It was a space designed for an agreement between parties, for the formalization of something that had already been decided somewhere else.

You do not worship what you have a contract with. You serve it. Or you break from it. Or it collects.

He keeps this thought close. It is not ready to be said aloud yet. But it is the most important thing he knows about [[Campaign Knowledge/Locations/The Sealed Ruin Beneath Grainfall|the sealed ruin]] and he knows it because his mother's crest was on that wall and she spent her life becoming the opposite of what it represented.

She did not worship something beneath the world. She refused it. And something came to her bedside the night before she died with a warm smile and circular glasses.

The ruins were not built to worship. They were built to remember an agreement. And someone is still honoring it.

---

### "The ancient civilization was destroyed by divine punishment."

His first instinct is the scholar's instinct. Civilizations are rarely destroyed by anything divine. They are destroyed by resource collapse, by disease, by internal fracture, by being on the wrong side of a migration pattern. The gods get credit for a lot of things that were already happening.

His second instinct, the one that comes after standing in that ceremonial room with his father's journal open to a page he was never meant to read, is more complicated.

Divine punishment implies something was done wrong. It implies a standard that was violated, a line that was crossed, a power that decided enough was enough. That framing assumes the destroying force was righteous and the destroyed civilization was not.

But his mother's crest was on that wall. And his mother was the most righteous person he ever knew. She was light and warmth and faith and choice and she wore [[Campaign Knowledge/Organizations/The Dawnmere Lineage|her bloodline's mark]] as a holy symbol and made it mean something better than what it was given to her to mean.

If her ancestors were destroyed by something calling itself divine, he wants to know who decided that. He wants to know what they did that warranted it. He wants to know if the destroying force and the thing that spoke to him in the ceremonial room are the same entity or different ones.

He does not say any of this. He simply nods when he hears the rumor and files it under things that need more information before he has an opinion worth sharing.

What he knows is that divine punishment is a story told by whoever survived. And survivors have reasons for the stories they tell.

---

### "People who enter the ruins don't come back... or come back wrong."

He came back. He is sitting in a jail cell in [[Campaign Knowledge/Locations/Grainfall]] with [[Campaign Knowledge/Characters/Ari]] on his knee and his mother's pendant glowing softly against his chest and he came back. That is something.

Whether he came back right is a question he is less certain about. He noticed the changes before he went into the ruin. Small things, subtle enough to dismiss, familiar enough to recognize because he had watched them happen to someone else for years. He knows this pattern. He watched it hollow out the most important person in his life over the course of a decade and he does not know yet whether what he is noticing in himself is the same thing or something adjacent to it or something else entirely.

His father went into those ruins and came back wrong over years. Slowly, incrementally, in ways that were easy to explain away until they were not. The reclusiveness, the bitterness, the anger that had no target, the way he pushed everyone away, the way he ran toward death in ways that were unlike his character.

[[Campaign Knowledge/Characters/Zephyrix Skydelver|Zeph]] went into the ruins once and ran out again in under an hour. He does not know what that means for the trajectory. He does not know if the brevity of the exposure matters or if the ritual changed the calculation entirely. He does not know if [[Campaign Knowledge/Characters/Ari]] and [[Campaign Knowledge/Items/Sylara's Amulet|the amulet]] activating in the same night is connected or coincidental.

What he knows is that he hears this rumor and he does not dismiss it the way the people saying it expect him to. He hears it and he thinks about his father. He thinks about [[Campaign Knowledge/Items/Aeralon's Journal|the journal]] pages that grew more erratic as they approached the present. He thinks about a single sentence written in perfect clarity in the middle of madness.

And then he thinks about himself. About what he is noticing. About the pattern he recognizes.

He came back. He is reasonably certain of that.

He came back right is the question he is going to spend this campaign answering.
