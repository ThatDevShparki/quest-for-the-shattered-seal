# Session 1

## What Happened

The full present-state narrative is told in [[Campaign Notes/Story/05 - The Cell|The Cell]]. Session 1 begins in the jail cell in [[Grainfall]]. [[Ari]] on his knee. [[Sylara's Amulet|The amulet]] warm. [[Aeralon's Journal|The journal]] open. The party asleep around him. Morning approaching.

The room is stone walls that get damp from the inside, a low ceiling, the smell of cold and age. A torch at the end of a corridor throws just enough light through the bars to see by without quite being enough to read by. He has not tried to read. He has been thinking instead.

---

## What He Learned

Nothing new yet. Session 1 has not been played. Everything [[Zephyrix Skydelver|Zeph]] carries into this moment comes from the backstory established in Session 0 and the Campaign Story.

---

## What Changed

He is exhausted in the way that comes from a mind working at full capacity without pause since the ritual. Too many answers rather than too few. Every piece of information connects to three others and the picture keeps getting larger rather than clearer. [[Aeralon's Journal|The journal]], [[Sylara's Amulet|the amulet]], [[Ari]], [[The Visage]], [[The Dawnmere Lineage|the Dawnmere crest]] on the wall, the tapestry in [[The Ruined Observatory|the Observatory]], [[The Four Symbols]], [[Sae-th]], [[The Brothers]], his mother's voice in something that was not his mother. He is holding all of it simultaneously and the holding is expensive.

But beneath the exhaustion and the overwhelm, something quiet. He went into that ruin and something ancient spoke to him in his mother's voice and asked him to kneel. He ran. He is not proud of that. But he is still here. Still holding [[Aeralon's Journal|the journal]] his father left him. Still wearing his mother's crest. Still himself, which is more than [[Aeralon Ashdelve|Aeralon]] managed in that room, and [[Aeralon Ashdelve|Aeralon]] was the smartest person [[Zephyrix Skydelver|Zeph]] has ever known.

He reacted once. He is not going to react again.

---

## What He Is Thinking About

[[The Visage]]. It is the loudest thing in his mind and has been since the moment it spoke.

Not the chaos of the flight or the arrest or the disbelief of the authorities or even the emergence of [[Ari]] from [[Aeralon's Journal|the journal]]. Those things happened and he processed them and they are filed. [[The Visage]] has not filed. It sits at the front of his mind in the specific way that unanswered questions sit when they are the kind that do not allow themselves to be set aside.

It offered him knowledge. Of his mother. Of secrets his father kept. It spoke in [[Sylara Dawnmere|Sylara]]'s voice without her warmth and it was desperate beneath the asking and it woke [[Sylara's Amulet|the amulet]] and [[Ari]] as proof of concept and then he ran without answering.

He did not say yes. He did not say no. He ran. And whatever is in that ceremonial room has been patient for long enough that a little more patience is nothing to it.

The question is still open. He knows this. He does not know yet what his answer will be. He knows the answer is coming.

He is also thinking about his mother. He has been thinking about his mother since he read her note on the first page of [[Aeralon's Journal|the journal]] in the cold house before he left, and the thinking has not stopped and has only intensified with everything he has found since. She is at the center of all of this. [[The Dawnmere Lineage|The Dawnmere crest]] on the wall, the tapestry in [[The Ruined Observatory|the Observatory]], [[The Visage]] speaking in her voice, [[Ari]] saying her family name in the dark. She knew what she came from. She chose differently. He does not yet know what she chose it over and he needs to know and he is going to find out.

He is thinking about [[Ari]] and what the phrases mean and whether he is ever going to learn to hear what it is actually trying to say.

He is thinking about the blank pages at the back of [[Aeralon's Journal|the journal]] and what he will write in them when he understands enough to write.

He is thinking, quietly and privately and not yet ready to examine it directly, that he might be connected to one of [[The Brothers]] through his mother's bloodline. He is not looking at this thought head on yet. He is aware it is there.

## What He Looks Like

A young Aarakocra, slight and rumpled, spectacles slightly askew. Tawny gold and cream feathers with dust still worked into the plumage from [[The Sealed Ruin Beneath Grainfall|the sealed ruin]]. Mud dried on one cheek feather from a moment in the flight out that he does not fully remember. The feather crest at the back of his head is doing what it always does, standing at an angle that suggests someone who has not looked in a mirror and would not think to.

[[Aeralon's Journal|The journal]] is open on his lap. [[Sylara's Amulet|The amulet]] is around his neck. [[Ari]] is on his knee. Three inheritances, all visible, none of them fully understood.

He looks like exactly what he is. A twenty three year old scholar who crawled through a ruin and came out the other side of something he does not yet have words for, sitting in a jail cell in [[Grainfall]], waiting for morning and whatever comes after it.

He does not look emboldened. That part is internal. It will surface when the moment requires it.

---

## What Comes Next

Morning. Whatever the authorities decide to do with him. The party he is about to meet, who are in this cell with him right now, some of them asleep and some of them not, none of them yet knowing what he carries or what he has been inside or what spoke to him in the dark of a ceremonial room three days ago.

[[The Ruined Observatory]] is still at the edge of the forest. [[The Sealed Ruin Beneath Grainfall|The sealed ruin]] is still beneath the town. [[The Visage]] is still in the ceremonial room or connected to it or waiting in whatever way ancient things wait. [[Aeralon's Journal|The journal]] has blank pages. [[Sylara's Amulet|The amulet]] has more tiers. [[Ari]] has more to say if he can learn to hear it. [[The Four Symbols]] have three other bearers somewhere in the world. [[Sae-th]] has passages he cannot read yet. [[The Brothers]] exist. [[The Architect]] came to his mother's bedside and gave a title instead of a name.

He has been in [[Grainfall]] for three days. It already feels like the most important place he has ever been.

He is twenty three years old and freshly alone in the world and he will not say either of those things out loud.

But he is emboldened. Quietly, privately, in a way that has not yet had occasion to show itself.

Whatever is in that bloodline, whatever spoke to him in that room, whatever his mother chose and whatever his father found, it has been here the whole time. It has always been here. And it did not break him in that ceremonial room.

That means something. He is not yet sure what. But he is going to find out.

_Dawnmere-burns._

He is still here.
