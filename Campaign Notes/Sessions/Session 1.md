# Present State

## The Room

A cell. Stone walls that get damp from the inside, the particular dampness of old stone that has not been warm in years. The floor is the same stone as the walls and the ceiling is low enough that he is aware of it. It smells of cold and age and faintly of something mineral that he recognizes from excavation sites and tries not to think about too hard.

The light is minimal. A torch somewhere at the end of a corridor throws just enough illumination through the bars to see by without quite being enough to read by. He has not tried to read. He has been thinking instead, which requires no light at all.

Most of the others in the cell are asleep or close enough to it that the difference does not matter. The guards have stopped making rounds with any regularity. The city outside has gone quiet in the way cities go quiet very late, the noise retreating to somewhere that feels like another world.

---

## What He Is Doing

He is sitting with his back against the stone wall. Ari is on his knee.

The journal is open across his lap to a page near the end, one of the pages where the handwriting was still steady enough to be deliberate, the annotation about Sae-th. He has read it many times tonight. He is not reading it now. He is looking at it without seeing it, which is different.

Sylara's amulet is around his neck. It catches the faint torchlight at the end of the corridor and holds it slightly longer than it should. He has stopped noticing this consciously. It is simply something the amulet does now. It is warm against his chest in the way it has been warm since the ceremonial room. Not hot. Warm. He has stopped noticing this consciously too.

Ari is calm. The raven sits on his knee with the particular stillness of something that has been patient for a very long time and has not lost the habit. Its eyes carry the quality of attention he noticed the first time it looked at him and has not been able to stop noticing since. Not the way birds watch. Something older.

It has had two spurts since the cell went quiet. Both times the same. _Never-dark._ Flight. _Dawnmere-burns._ Stillness. He watched both times and understood neither time and is keeping both carefully in the part of his mind where he keeps things that are important before he knows why.

---

## His Emotional Temperature

**Exhausted.**

Not the exhaustion of physical effort, though his body has that too, the ruin and the flight and the arrest and the long night of no sleep. The exhaustion of someone whose mind has been working at full capacity without pause since he performed the ritual and something spoke back. He has not stopped processing. He does not know how to stop processing. His father's son.

**Overwhelmed.**

Too many answers rather than too few. That is the specific quality of his overwhelm. He came to Grainfall with questions and the questions have not been answered so much as multiplied. Every piece of information he has found in the last three days connects to three others and the picture keeps getting larger rather than clearer. The journal, the amulet, Ari, the Visage, the crest on the wall, the tapestry in the Observatory, the four symbols, Sae-th, the Brothers, his mother's voice in something that was not his mother. He is holding all of it simultaneously and the holding is expensive.

**Searching.**

This has not stopped and will not stop. He is a scholar trained by the most thorough scholar he ever knew and the not knowing is always worse than the knowing or at least that is what he has believed his entire life. Grainfall is beginning to complicate that belief. He is searching anyway. The journal, the amulet, Ari, the phrases. Turning everything over in his mind the way his hands turned the amulet in the dark earlier tonight, looking for the shape of what it adds up to.

**Emboldened.**

This is the quiet one. The one nobody in this cell can see yet and that he has not said aloud to anyone. He went into that ruin and something ancient spoke to him in his mother's voice and asked him to kneel. He ran. He is not proud of that. But he is still here. Still holding the journal his father left him. Still wearing his mother's crest. Still himself, which is more than Aeralon managed in that room, and Aeralon was the smartest person Zeph has ever known.

He reacted once. He is not going to react again.

---

## What He Is Thinking About

The Visage. It is the loudest thing in his mind and has been since the moment it spoke.

Not the chaos of the flight or the arrest or the disbelief of the authorities or even the emergence of Ari from the journal. Those things happened and he processed them and they are filed. The Visage has not filed. It sits at the front of his mind in the specific way that unanswered questions sit when they are the kind that do not allow themselves to be set aside.

It offered him knowledge. Of his mother. Of secrets his father kept. It spoke in Sylara's voice without her warmth and it was desperate beneath the asking and it woke the amulet and the raven as proof of concept and then he ran without answering.

He did not say yes. He did not say no. He ran. And whatever is in that ceremonial room has been patient for long enough that a little more patience is nothing to it.

The question is still open. He knows this. He does not know yet what his answer will be. He knows the answer is coming.

He is also thinking about his mother. He has been thinking about his mother since he read her note on the first page of the journal in the cold house before he left, and the thinking has not stopped and has only intensified with everything he has found since. She is at the center of all of this. The crest on the wall, the tapestry in the Observatory, the Visage speaking in her voice, Ari saying her family name in the dark. She knew what she came from. She chose differently. He does not yet know what she chose it over and he needs to know and he is going to find out.

He is thinking about Ari and what the phrases mean and whether he is ever going to learn to hear what it is actually trying to say.

He is thinking about the blank pages at the back of the journal and what he will write in them when he understands enough to write.

He is thinking, quietly and privately and not yet ready to examine it directly, that he might be connected to one of the Brothers through his mother's bloodline. He is not looking at this thought head on yet. He is aware it is there.

---

## What He Looks Like

A young Aarakocra, slight and rumpled, spectacles slightly askew. Tawny gold and cream feathers with dust still worked into the plumage from the ruin. Mud dried on one cheek feather from a moment in the flight out that he does not fully remember. The feather crest at the back of his head is doing what it always does, standing at an angle that suggests someone who has not looked in a mirror and would not think to.

The journal is open on his lap. The amulet is around his neck. The raven is on his knee. Three inheritances, all visible, none of them fully understood.

He looks like exactly what he is. A twenty three year old scholar who crawled through a ruin and came out the other side of something he does not yet have words for, sitting in a jail cell in Grainfall, waiting for morning and whatever comes after it.

He does not look emboldened. That part is internal. It will surface when the moment requires it.

---

## What Comes Next

Morning. Whatever the authorities decide to do with him. The party he is about to meet, who are in this cell with him right now, some of them asleep and some of them not, none of them yet knowing what he carries or what he has been inside or what spoke to him in the dark of a ceremonial room three days ago.

The Ruined Observatory is still at the edge of the forest. The sealed ruin is still beneath the town. The Visage is still in the ceremonial room or connected to it or waiting in whatever way ancient things wait. The journal has blank pages. The amulet has more tiers. Ari has more to say if he can learn to hear it. The four symbols have three other bearers somewhere in the world. Sae-th has passages he cannot read yet. The Brothers exist. The Architect came to his mother's bedside and gave a title instead of a name.

He has been in Grainfall for three days. It already feels like the most important place he has ever been.

He is twenty three years old and freshly alone in the world and he will not say either of those things out loud.

But he is emboldened. Quietly, privately, in a way that has not yet had occasion to show itself.

Whatever is in that bloodline, whatever spoke to him in that room, whatever his mother chose and whatever his father found, it has been here the whole time. It has always been here. And it did not break him in that ceremonial room.

That means something. He is not yet sure what. But he is going to find out.

_Dawnmere-burns._

He is still here.
