# Session 1

## What Happened

The full present-state narrative is told in [[Campaign Notes/Story/05 - The Cell|The Cell]]. Session 1 begins in the jail cell in [[Campaign Knowledge/Locations/Grainfall]]. [[Campaign Knowledge/Characters/Ari]] on his knee. [[Campaign Knowledge/Items/Sylara's Amulet|The amulet]] warm. [[Campaign Knowledge/Items/Aeralon's Journal|The journal]] open. The party asleep around him. Morning approaching.

The room is stone walls that get damp from the inside, a low ceiling, the smell of cold and age. A torch at the end of a corridor throws just enough light through the bars to see by without quite being enough to read by. He has not tried to read. He has been thinking instead.

---

## What He Learned

The name came back. [[Campaign Knowledge/Characters/The Architect]]. Eighteen years after a warm smile at his mother's bedside, the same name out of [[Campaign Knowledge/Characters/Ezekiel|Zeke's]] mouth in a jail cell in [[Campaign Knowledge/Locations/Grainfall]]. The older man, [[Campaign Knowledge/Characters/Ra]], who had been coughing blood and speaking in a voice that did not match what they heard, told Zeke his name was the Architect. The connection was immediate. The shock was total. [[Campaign Knowledge/Characters/Zephyrix Skydelver|Zeph]] has never connected malintent or mystery to his mother's death. The Architect was always a warm memory. Now the emotional frame has cracked.

A page is missing from [[Campaign Knowledge/Items/Aeralon's Journal|the journal]]. He noticed it when checking after hearing the name. The journal is sacred. His father's life work. Someone took something from it and that feels like a desecration.

[[Campaign Knowledge/Characters/Miss DuBois]] is from [[Campaign Knowledge/Organizations/The Orryn Society]] and knew [[Campaign Knowledge/Characters/Aeralon Ashdelve|Aeralon]]. She is the first living person Zeph has met who knew his father professionally. She was at a campsite with Aeralon near [[Campaign Knowledge/Locations/Grainfall]]. He had found something in the jungle and seemed crazed. Monsters attacked. She lost her leg. She does not know how either of them survived.

The pigs in the Turning of the Veil parade were injected with something via the stomach. They ate a man and a child. [[Campaign Knowledge/Characters/Calis]] recognized the child. The Diamond Trading Company brings animals from the mainland. [[Campaign Knowledge/Characters/Ezekiel|Zeke]] said the Architect uses syringes. [[Campaign Knowledge/Characters/Malcarion|Mal]] said the company brings in monsters. Syringes. Injected pigs. The Architect. These are not coincidences.

[[Campaign Knowledge/Locations/Grainfall]] is an island called Gossier. The Spinewater River runs through the east. The storm rarely stops. The river ran red with blood during the festival. Gold dissolves in the water. The locals call this normal.

DuBois gave them a map with three locations: Cibola, the Drowned Library, and a campsite ten miles from town. Quest: go to the campsite.

---

## What Changed

Before this morning, [[Campaign Knowledge/Characters/Zephyrix Skydelver|Zeph]] was alone with his father's journal and his mother's mysteries. Now he has four companions he did not choose, a quest from a dying woman who knew [[Campaign Knowledge/Characters/Aeralon Ashdelve|Aeralon]], a name he has not heard since childhood spoken by a bleeding man in a cell, a violated journal, and a town that is stranger and older and more layered than he understood from the inside of a ruin.

The Architect. That is the thing that changed most. The warm memory at the foundation of his understanding of [[Campaign Knowledge/Characters/Sylara Dawnmere|his mother's]] death, the detail he carried without questioning, is no longer simple. It is not yet frightening. It is confused in a way that goes deeper than frightening, because it reaches into the part of him that never thought to question how his mother died.

He is no longer working alone. Whether the party will understand what he carries or care about where the trail leads, he does not know. But they are walking with him toward the campsite, and for the first time since he left the cold house he is not the only person moving in this direction.

## What He Is Thinking About

[[Campaign Knowledge/Characters/The Visage]]. It is the loudest thing in his mind and has been since the moment it spoke.

Not the chaos of the flight or the arrest or the disbelief of the authorities or even the emergence of [[Campaign Knowledge/Characters/Ari]] from [[Campaign Knowledge/Items/Aeralon's Journal|the journal]]. Those things happened and he processed them and they are filed. [[Campaign Knowledge/Characters/The Visage]] has not filed. It sits at the front of his mind in the specific way that unanswered questions sit when they are the kind that do not allow themselves to be set aside.

It offered him knowledge. Of his mother. Of secrets his father kept. It spoke in [[Campaign Knowledge/Characters/Sylara Dawnmere|Sylara]]'s voice without her warmth and it was desperate beneath the asking and it woke [[Campaign Knowledge/Items/Sylara's Amulet|the amulet]] and [[Campaign Knowledge/Characters/Ari]] as proof of concept and then he ran without answering.

He did not say yes. He did not say no. He ran. And whatever is in that ceremonial room has been patient for long enough that a little more patience is nothing to it.

The question is still open. He knows this. He does not know yet what his answer will be. He knows the answer is coming.

He is also thinking about his mother. He has been thinking about his mother since he read her note on the first page of [[Campaign Knowledge/Items/Aeralon's Journal|the journal]] in the cold house before he left, and the thinking has not stopped and has only intensified with everything he has found since. She is at the center of all of this. [[Campaign Knowledge/Organizations/The Dawnmere Lineage|The Dawnmere crest]] on the wall, the tapestry in [[Campaign Knowledge/Locations/The Ruined Observatory|the Observatory]], [[Campaign Knowledge/Characters/The Visage]] speaking in her voice, [[Campaign Knowledge/Characters/Ari]] saying her family name in the dark. She knew what she came from. She chose differently. He does not yet know what she chose it over and he needs to know and he is going to find out.

He is thinking about [[Campaign Knowledge/Characters/Ari]] and what the phrases mean and whether he is ever going to learn to hear what it is actually trying to say.

He is thinking about the blank pages at the back of [[Campaign Knowledge/Items/Aeralon's Journal|the journal]] and what he will write in them when he understands enough to write.

He is thinking, quietly and privately and not yet ready to examine it directly, that he might be connected to one of [[Campaign Knowledge/Clues/The Brothers]] through his mother's bloodline. He is not looking at this thought head on yet. He is aware it is there.

## What He Looks Like

A young Aarakocra, slight and rumpled, spectacles slightly askew. Tawny gold and cream feathers with dust still worked into the plumage from [[Campaign Knowledge/Locations/The Sealed Ruin Beneath Grainfall|the sealed ruin]]. Mud dried on one cheek feather from a moment in the flight out that he does not fully remember. The feather crest at the back of his head is doing what it always does, standing at an angle that suggests someone who has not looked in a mirror and would not think to.

[[Campaign Knowledge/Items/Aeralon's Journal|The journal]] is open on his lap. [[Campaign Knowledge/Items/Sylara's Amulet|The amulet]] is around his neck. [[Campaign Knowledge/Characters/Ari]] is on his knee. Three inheritances, all visible, none of them fully understood.

He looks like exactly what he is. A twenty three year old scholar who crawled through a ruin and came out the other side of something he does not yet have words for, sitting in a jail cell in [[Campaign Knowledge/Locations/Grainfall]], waiting for morning and whatever comes after it.

He does not look emboldened. That part is internal. It will surface when the moment requires it.

---

## What Comes Next

Morning. Whatever the authorities decide to do with him. The party he is about to meet, who are in this cell with him right now, some of them asleep and some of them not, none of them yet knowing what he carries or what he has been inside or what spoke to him in the dark of a ceremonial room three days ago.

[[Campaign Knowledge/Locations/The Ruined Observatory]] is still at the edge of the forest. [[Campaign Knowledge/Locations/The Sealed Ruin Beneath Grainfall|The sealed ruin]] is still beneath the town. [[Campaign Knowledge/Characters/The Visage]] is still in the ceremonial room or connected to it or waiting in whatever way ancient things wait. [[Campaign Knowledge/Items/Aeralon's Journal|The journal]] has blank pages. [[Campaign Knowledge/Items/Sylara's Amulet|The amulet]] has more tiers. [[Campaign Knowledge/Characters/Ari]] has more to say if he can learn to hear it. [[Campaign Knowledge/Clues/The Four Symbols]] have three other bearers somewhere in the world. [[Campaign Knowledge/Clues/Sae-th]] has passages he cannot read yet. [[Campaign Knowledge/Clues/The Brothers]] exist. [[Campaign Knowledge/Characters/The Architect]] came to his mother's bedside and gave a title instead of a name.

He has been in [[Campaign Knowledge/Locations/Grainfall]] for three days. It already feels like the most important place he has ever been.

He is twenty three years old and freshly alone in the world and he will not say either of those things out loud.

But he is emboldened. Quietly, privately, in a way that has not yet had occasion to show itself.

Whatever is in that bloodline, whatever spoke to him in that room, whatever his mother chose and whatever his father found, it has been here the whole time. It has always been here. And it did not break him in that ceremonial room.

That means something. He is not yet sure what. But he is going to find out.

_Dawnmere-burns._

He is still here.
