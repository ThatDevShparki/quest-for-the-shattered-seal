# The Ritual

He did not stop.

[[Aeralon Ashdelve|His father]] had written of this room in his earlier years, before the madness. Clean and precise descriptions of a ceremonial space matching this one exactly, cross referenced with a ritual [[Aeralon Ashdelve|Aeralon]] had found in texts so obscure they were considered fiction by most scholars. A ritual of the dead. A calling, designed to reach gods long forgotten by the living world.

[[Aeralon Ashdelve|Aeralon]] had been infected when he stood here. [[Aeralon Ashdelve|Aeralon]] had been losing himself. [[Zephyrix Skydelver|Zeph]] was not. He was clear headed, making clean connections, reading the room the way his father had taught him to read every room. He understood what he was looking at. And he understood, with the particular certainty of someone who has spent his life being his father's son, that his father had been too afraid to finish what he started.

He performed the ritual alone in [[The Sealed Ruin Beneath Grainfall|the ceremonial room]].

Something spoke back.

A woman's voice. At first he thought it was [[Sylara Dawnmere|his mother]]. The timbre of it, the cadence, something in the shape of the sound reached directly into his earliest memories and pulled. But it was not her. It was something that knew exactly how to sound like her, something that had found the most unguarded place in him and pressed itself against it like a key against a lock.

[[The Visage|It]] asked him to become its servant.

He ran.

No answer, no refusal, simply ran. The room erupted behind him. Skeletons rising from crypts, walls shaking, braziers igniting, urns shattering against the stone floor. The ground heaved beneath him the entire length of [[The Sealed Ruin Beneath Grainfall|the ruin]] and he did not slow once, did not look back, ran straight through the entrance and into the street outside and grabbed the first person he found and told them that a god had awoken in the ruins beneath [[Grainfall]].

When the authorities went to look, [[The Sealed Ruin Beneath Grainfall|the ruin]] was exactly as it had always been. Cobwebs intact across every surface. Urns unbroken. Skeletons resting peacefully in their crypts. No sign of disturbance. No sign of anyone having been inside at all.

He was arrested for trespassing in a protected site and, in the eyes of everyone who heard his account, for being dangerously and publicly out of his mind.

In the chaos of running, [[Sylara's Amulet|the amulet]] grew warm against his chest for the first time. Not hot. Warm. Like a hand on a shoulder in a dark room.
