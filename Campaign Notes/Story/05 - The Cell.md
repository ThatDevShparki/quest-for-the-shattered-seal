# The Cell

A cell. Stone walls that get damp from the inside, the particular dampness of old stone that has not been warm in years. The floor is the same stone as the walls and the ceiling is low enough that he is aware of it. It smells of cold and age and faintly of something mineral that he recognizes from excavation sites and tries not to think about too hard.

The light is minimal. A torch somewhere at the end of a corridor throws just enough illumination through the bars to see by without quite being enough to read by. He has not tried to read. He has been thinking instead, which requires no light at all.

Most of the others in the cell are asleep or close enough to it that the difference does not matter. The guards have stopped making rounds with any regularity. The city outside has gone quiet, the noise retreating to somewhere that feels like another world.

---

Sometime in the darkest part of that night, [[Campaign Knowledge/Items/Aeralon's Journal|the journal]] moved.

Not much. A tremor along the cover, like something breathing inside it. He had been turning [[Campaign Knowledge/Items/Sylara's Amulet|the pendant]] over in his hands, testing what he had felt, watching it catch light that was not there. The amulet had been warm since [[Campaign Knowledge/Locations/The Sealed Ruin Beneath Grainfall|the ceremonial room]], a steady heat that did not fade as hours passed, that should have cooled against the cold stone of the cell and did not. He noticed the journal's tremor the way you notice something at the edge of your attention when everything else has gone quiet.

Then [[Campaign Knowledge/Characters/Ari|a raven]] pulled itself out of the pages like smoke finding a shape. It landed on his knee, ruffled its feathers once, and looked at him with eyes that carried something he almost recognized. Not [[Campaign Knowledge/Characters/Sylara Dawnmere|his mother]]. Not [[Campaign Knowledge/Characters/The Visage|the voice]] from the ritual. Something older and closer and quieter than either. When it spoke, it spoke in [[Campaign Knowledge/Characters/Aeralon Ashdelve|his father's]] cadences. Fragments. Observations about stonework. Notes about burial rites in the third age. Half finished sentences from field journals [[Campaign Knowledge/Characters/Zephyrix Skydelver|Zeph]] had never been allowed to read.

[[Campaign Knowledge/Characters/Aeralon Ashdelve|Aeralon]] had a familiar his entire career. It had been in [[Campaign Knowledge/Items/Aeralon's Journal|the journal]] the whole time. Waiting in the only place it had left to wait.

His inheritance was never just [[Campaign Knowledge/Items/Aeralon's Journal|the journal]]. It was what lived inside it.

---

He is sitting with his back against the stone wall. [[Campaign Knowledge/Characters/Ari]] is on his knee.

[[Campaign Knowledge/Items/Aeralon's Journal|The journal]] is open across his lap to a page near the end, one of the pages where the handwriting was still steady enough to be deliberate, the annotation about [[Campaign Knowledge/Clues/Sae-th]]. He has read it many times tonight. He is not reading it now. He is looking at it without seeing it, which is different.

[[Campaign Knowledge/Items/Sylara's Amulet|Sylara's amulet]] is around his neck. It has not cooled since the ritual. In the faint torchlight at the end of the corridor, the crest catches and holds the light at angles that should not work, that do not correspond to any geometry he can account for. He noticed it the first time an hour ago. He has noticed it four times since. Each time the light lingers a fraction longer, as though the pendant is learning what it can do, or remembering.

He does not know what it means. He knows the amulet was an object for the weeks he wore it before the ritual and is something else now. Something patient and unhurried, the stillness of old things recognizing that a specific moment has finally arrived. His mother wore it as a holy symbol of light. Whatever it is becoming in the dark of this cell, it was something before that.

---

[[Campaign Knowledge/Characters/Ari]] is calm. The raven sits on his knee with the particular stillness of something that has been patient for a very long time and has not lost the habit. Its eyes carry the quality of attention he noticed the first time it looked at him and has not been able to stop noticing since. Not the way birds watch. Something older.

It has had two spurts since the cell went quiet. Both times the same. _Never-dark._ Flight. _Dawnmere-burns._ Stillness. He watched both times and understood neither time and is keeping both carefully in the part of his mind where he keeps things that are important before he knows why.

---

[[Campaign Knowledge/Characters/The Visage]]. It is the loudest thing in his mind and has been since the moment it spoke. Not the chaos of the flight, not the arrest, not even the emergence of [[Campaign Knowledge/Characters/Ari]] from [[Campaign Knowledge/Items/Aeralon's Journal|the journal]]. Those things happened and he processed them and they are filed. The Visage has not filed.

It offered him knowledge and he ran before he could learn whether the offer was real. It spoke in [[Campaign Knowledge/Characters/Sylara Dawnmere|Sylara's]] voice without her warmth. It knew things about his mother that he does not know. It named his father's burden without revealing it. Then it asked for something he was not ready to give. The word was servant. The feeling beneath the word was need. And whatever is in [[Campaign Knowledge/Locations/The Sealed Ruin Beneath Grainfall|that ceremonial room]] has been patient for long enough that a little more patience is nothing to it.

He is thinking about [[Campaign Knowledge/Characters/Sylara Dawnmere|his mother]]. He has been thinking about her since he read her note on the first page of [[Campaign Knowledge/Items/Aeralon's Journal|the journal]] in the cold house before he left. She is at the center of all of this. The crest on the wall, the tapestry in [[Campaign Knowledge/Locations/The Ruined Observatory|the Observatory]], [[Campaign Knowledge/Characters/The Visage]] speaking in her voice, [[Campaign Knowledge/Characters/Ari]] saying her family name in the dark. She knew what she came from. She chose differently. He does not yet know what she chose it over.

He is thinking about [[Campaign Knowledge/Characters/Ari]] and whether he will learn to hear what it is trying to say. About the blank pages at the back of [[Campaign Knowledge/Items/Aeralon's Journal|the journal]] and what he will write in them when he understands enough to write. About the possibility, quiet and unexamined, that he might be connected to one of [[Campaign Knowledge/Clues/The Brothers]] through [[Campaign Knowledge/Characters/Sylara Dawnmere|his mother's]] bloodline. He is not looking at that thought head on yet. He is aware it is there.

---

A young Aarakocra, slight and rumpled, spectacles slightly askew. Tawny gold and cream feathers with dust still worked into the plumage from [[Campaign Knowledge/Locations/The Sealed Ruin Beneath Grainfall|the ruin]]. Mud dried on one cheek feather from a moment in the flight out that he does not fully remember. The feather crest at the back of his head is doing what it always does, standing at an angle that suggests someone who has not looked in a mirror and would not think to.

[[Campaign Knowledge/Items/Aeralon's Journal|The journal]] is open on his lap. [[Campaign Knowledge/Items/Sylara's Amulet|The amulet]] is around his neck. [[Campaign Knowledge/Characters/Ari|The raven]] is on his knee. Three inheritances, all visible, none of them fully understood.

He does not look emboldened. That part is internal. It will surface when the moment requires it.

---

Morning. Whatever the authorities decide to do with him. The party he is about to meet, who are in this cell with him right now, some of them asleep and some of them not, none of them yet knowing what he carries or what he has been inside or what spoke to him in the dark of [[Campaign Knowledge/Locations/The Sealed Ruin Beneath Grainfall|a ceremonial room]] three days ago.

[[Campaign Knowledge/Locations/The Ruined Observatory]] is still at the edge of the forest. [[Campaign Knowledge/Locations/The Sealed Ruin Beneath Grainfall|The sealed ruin]] is still beneath the town. [[Campaign Knowledge/Characters/The Visage]] is still in [[Campaign Knowledge/Locations/The Sealed Ruin Beneath Grainfall|the ceremonial room]] or connected to it or waiting in whatever way ancient things wait. [[Campaign Knowledge/Items/Aeralon's Journal|The journal]] has blank pages. [[Campaign Knowledge/Items/Sylara's Amulet|The amulet]] holds more than it has shown him. [[Campaign Knowledge/Characters/Ari]] has more to say if he can learn to hear it. [[Campaign Knowledge/Clues/The Four Symbols]] have three other bearers somewhere in the world. [[Campaign Knowledge/Clues/Sae-th]] has passages he cannot read yet. [[Campaign Knowledge/Clues/The Brothers]] exist. [[Campaign Knowledge/Characters/The Architect]] came to [[Campaign Knowledge/Characters/Sylara Dawnmere|his mother's]] bedside and gave a title instead of a name.

He is not proud of running. His mother would not have run. She would have stood in that room and looked at whatever was speaking and made her choice with both eyes open. He is going back. And this time he is going to answer.

He has been in [[Campaign Knowledge/Locations/Grainfall]] for three days. It already feels like the most important place he has ever been.

He is twenty three years old and freshly alone in the world and he will not say either of those things out loud.

Whatever is in [[Campaign Knowledge/Organizations/The Dawnmere Lineage|that bloodline]], whatever spoke to him in that room, whatever [[Campaign Knowledge/Characters/Sylara Dawnmere|his mother]] chose and whatever [[Campaign Knowledge/Characters/Aeralon Ashdelve|his father]] found, it has been here the whole time. It has always been here. And it did not break him in [[Campaign Knowledge/Locations/The Sealed Ruin Beneath Grainfall|that ceremonial room]].

That means something. He is not yet sure what. But he is going to find out.

_Dawnmere-burns._

He is still here.
