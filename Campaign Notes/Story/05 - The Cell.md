# The Cell

He spent the night in the cell turning [[Sylara's Amulet|the pendant]] over in the dark. Testing what he had felt. Watching it catch light that was not there. And sometime in the darkest part of that night, [[Aeralon's Journal|the journal]] moved.

Not much. A tremor along the cover, like something breathing inside it.

Then [[Ari|a raven]] pulled itself out of the pages like smoke finding a shape. It landed on his knee, ruffled its feathers once, and looked at him with eyes that carried something he almost recognized. Not [[Sylara Dawnmere|his mother]]. Not [[The Visage|the voice]] from the ritual. Something older and closer and quieter than either. When it spoke, it spoke in [[Aeralon Ashdelve|his father's]] cadences. Fragments. Observations about stonework. Notes about burial rites in the third age. Half finished sentences from field journals [[Zephyrix Skydelver|Zeph]] had never been allowed to read.

[[Aeralon Ashdelve|Aeralon]] had a familiar his entire career. It had been in [[Aeralon's Journal|the journal]] the whole time. Waiting in the only place it had left to wait.

His inheritance was never just [[Aeralon's Journal|the journal]]. It was what lived inside it.

He knows what he heard in that room. He knows what it felt like. And he knows that he did not say no. He simply ran. And whatever is waiting in [[The Dawnmere Lineage|that bloodline]], in that room, in [[The Visage|the voice]] that knew exactly how to sound like [[Sylara Dawnmere]], it knows that too.

He is not proud of running. His mother would not have run. She would have stood in that room and looked at whatever was speaking and made her choice with both eyes open. He is going back. And this time he is going to answer.

---

A cell. Stone walls that get damp from the inside, the particular dampness of old stone that has not been warm in years. The floor is the same stone as the walls and the ceiling is low enough that he is aware of it. It smells of cold and age and faintly of something mineral that he recognizes from excavation sites and tries not to think about too hard.

The light is minimal. A torch somewhere at the end of a corridor throws just enough illumination through the bars to see by without quite being enough to read by. He has not tried to read. He has been thinking instead, which requires no light at all.

Most of the others in the cell are asleep or close enough to it that the difference does not matter. The guards have stopped making rounds with any regularity. The city outside has gone quiet in the way cities go quiet very late, the noise retreating to somewhere that feels like another world.

He is sitting with his back against the stone wall. [[Ari]] is on his knee.

[[Aeralon's Journal|The journal]] is open across his lap to a page near the end, one of the pages where the handwriting was still steady enough to be deliberate, the annotation about [[Sae-th]]. He has read it many times tonight. He is not reading it now. He is looking at it without seeing it, which is different.

[[Sylara's Amulet|Sylara's amulet]] is around his neck. It catches the faint torchlight at the end of the corridor and holds it slightly longer than it should. He has stopped noticing this consciously. It is simply something [[Sylara's Amulet|the amulet]] does now. It is warm against his chest in the way it has been warm since [[The Sealed Ruin Beneath Grainfall|the ceremonial room]]. Not hot. Warm. He has stopped noticing this consciously too.

[[Ari]] is calm. The raven sits on his knee with the particular stillness of something that has been patient for a very long time and has not lost the habit. Its eyes carry the quality of attention he noticed the first time it looked at him and has not been able to stop noticing since. Not the way birds watch. Something older.

It has had two spurts since the cell went quiet. Both times the same. _Never-dark._ Flight. _Dawnmere-burns._ Stillness. He watched both times and understood neither time and is keeping both carefully in the part of his mind where he keeps things that are important before he knows why.

---

[[The Visage]]. It is the loudest thing in his mind and has been since the moment it spoke.

Not the chaos of the flight or the arrest or the disbelief of the authorities or even the emergence of [[Ari]] from [[Aeralon's Journal|the journal]]. Those things happened and he processed them and they are filed. [[The Visage]] has not filed. It sits at the front of his mind in the specific way that unanswered questions sit when they are the kind that do not allow themselves to be set aside.

It offered him knowledge. Of [[Sylara Dawnmere|his mother]]. Of secrets [[Aeralon Ashdelve|his father]] kept. It spoke in [[Sylara Dawnmere|Sylara's]] voice without her warmth and it was desperate beneath the asking and it woke [[Sylara's Amulet|the amulet]] and [[Ari|the raven]] as proof of concept and then he ran without answering.

He did not say yes. He did not say no. He ran. And whatever is in [[The Sealed Ruin Beneath Grainfall|that ceremonial room]] has been patient for long enough that a little more patience is nothing to it.

The question is still open. He knows this. He does not know yet what his answer will be. He knows the answer is coming.

He is also thinking about [[Sylara Dawnmere|his mother]]. He has been thinking about her since he read her note on the first page of [[Aeralon's Journal|the journal]] in the cold house before he left, and the thinking has not stopped and has only intensified with everything he has found since. She is at the center of all of this. The crest on the wall, the tapestry in [[The Ruined Observatory|the Observatory]], [[The Visage]] speaking in her voice, [[Ari]] saying her family name in the dark. She knew what she came from. She chose differently. He does not yet know what she chose it over and he needs to know and he is going to find out.

He is thinking about [[Ari]] and what the phrases mean and whether he is ever going to learn to hear what it is actually trying to say.

He is thinking about the blank pages at the back of [[Aeralon's Journal|the journal]] and what he will write in them when he understands enough to write.

He is thinking, quietly and privately and not yet ready to examine it directly, that he might be connected to one of [[The Brothers]] through [[Sylara Dawnmere|his mother's]] bloodline. He is not looking at this thought head on yet. He is aware it is there.

---

A young Aarakocra, slight and rumpled, spectacles slightly askew. Tawny gold and cream feathers with dust still worked into the plumage from [[The Sealed Ruin Beneath Grainfall|the ruin]]. Mud dried on one cheek feather from a moment in the flight out that he does not fully remember. The feather crest at the back of his head is doing what it always does, standing at an angle that suggests someone who has not looked in a mirror and would not think to.

[[Aeralon's Journal|The journal]] is open on his lap. [[Sylara's Amulet|The amulet]] is around his neck. [[Ari|The raven]] is on his knee. Three inheritances, all visible, none of them fully understood.

He looks like exactly what he is. A twenty three year old scholar who crawled through a ruin and came out the other side of something he does not yet have words for, sitting in a jail cell in [[Grainfall]], waiting for morning and whatever comes after it.

He does not look emboldened. That part is internal. It will surface when the moment requires it.

---

Morning. Whatever the authorities decide to do with him. The party he is about to meet, who are in this cell with him right now, some of them asleep and some of them not, none of them yet knowing what he carries or what he has been inside or what spoke to him in the dark of [[The Sealed Ruin Beneath Grainfall|a ceremonial room]] three days ago.

[[The Ruined Observatory]] is still at the edge of the forest. [[The Sealed Ruin Beneath Grainfall|The sealed ruin]] is still beneath the town. [[The Visage]] is still in [[The Sealed Ruin Beneath Grainfall|the ceremonial room]] or connected to it or waiting in whatever way ancient things wait. [[Aeralon's Journal|The journal]] has blank pages. [[Sylara's Amulet|The amulet]] has more tiers. [[Ari]] has more to say if he can learn to hear it. [[The Four Symbols]] have three other bearers somewhere in the world. [[Sae-th]] has passages he cannot read yet. [[The Brothers]] exist. [[The Architect]] came to [[Sylara Dawnmere|his mother's]] bedside and gave a title instead of a name.

He has been in [[Grainfall]] for three days. It already feels like the most important place he has ever been.

He is twenty three years old and freshly alone in the world and he will not say either of those things out loud.

But he is emboldened. Quietly, privately, in a way that has not yet had occasion to show itself.

Whatever is in [[The Dawnmere Lineage|that bloodline]], whatever spoke to him in that room, whatever [[Sylara Dawnmere|his mother]] chose and whatever [[Aeralon Ashdelve|his father]] found, it has been here the whole time. It has always been here. And it did not break him in [[The Sealed Ruin Beneath Grainfall|that ceremonial room]].

That means something. He is not yet sure what. But he is going to find out.

_Dawnmere-burns._

He is still here.
