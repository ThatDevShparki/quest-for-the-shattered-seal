# Arrival in Grainfall

[[Aeralon's Journal|Aeralon's journal]] was dense and meticulous in his earlier years. But toward the end the pages changed. The handwriting loosened, then fractured, then devolved into scribbles that covered margins and bled across diagrams. References to old gods, to rituals, to a place that matched descriptions [[Zephyrix Skydelver|Zeph]] had only ever seen in academic texts. A ruin considered lost. Considered mythological by most. And then, in the middle of it all, a single sentence written in perfect clarity.

> _"I found her, my love. After 20 years. [[Sylara Dawnmere|Sylara]] is here with me."_

At the edge of the forest just outside [[Grainfall]] stood a structure the locals called [[The Ruined Observatory]]. [[Zephyrix Skydelver|Zeph]] found it while orienting himself in the area, cross referencing his father's notes against the local geography. He noticed immediately that someone appeared to be living on the upper levels. Going further inside, he found the symbol of his house carved into the stone. He took down an inscription of the markings surrounding it and noticed that what had appeared to be a single symbol was in fact [[The Four Symbols|four symbols]], each remarkably similar to the others but with minor deliberate variations between them. He copied them carefully and moved on, filing the discovery under things that needed more context before they could be understood.

[[The Sealed Ruin Beneath Grainfall|The ruin beneath Grainfall]] had been sealed. Officially. Recently. The stated reason was structural instability. Nothing in his father's survey notes supported this. [[Zephyrix Skydelver|Zeph]] went in anyway.

[[The Sealed Ruin Beneath Grainfall|The ruin]] was extraordinary. Untouched, ancient, perfectly preserved in the way that only truly forgotten places are. Artifacts thick with dust. Crypts lining the walls with skeletons still clad in armor from centuries past, jeweled and armed and dressed in designs no living scholar had ever documented firsthand. And on every wall, in every hallway and chamber, murals. Detailed and deliberate, describing events and people and moments from a civilization that history had swallowed whole.

He found [[The Sealed Ruin Beneath Grainfall|the ceremonial room]] deep inside. A formal space, broad and vaulted, with braziers and altars arranged on a central platform. The walls here told a different story. Not events but lineage. A family tree rendered in careful detail, tracing fathers of kings and mothers of queens, cousins and distant relations sprawling outward across the stone in an unbroken web of bloodline.

And there, woven into that web, was [[The Dawnmere Lineage|the Dawnmere family crest]].

He recognized it immediately. The same crest on [[Sylara's Amulet|the amulet]] around his neck. The same crest his mother had worn her entire life. He stood looking at it for a long moment and then, with shaking hands, he opened [[Aeralon's Journal|the journal]].

He found the crest inside. Drawn over and over again, different sizes and orientations, some careful and some frantic. Every single one defaced. Crossed out, etched through, lines torn into the page with what must have been fury or fear or something that had stopped being one and become the other. [[Aeralon Ashdelve|His father]] had found this room too. Had stood in this same spot. Had drawn what he saw until he could not stop drawing it and then destroyed every trace of it he could reach.

And surrounding the defaced crests, threaded through the incoherent scribbles, were references to old gods and to a language called [[Sae-th]] and to something ancient connected to this place and [[The Dawnmere Lineage|this bloodline]] that [[Aeralon Ashdelve|Aeralon]] had clearly understood and just as clearly did not want his son to find.

It was not the crest on the wall that disturbed him. It was the realization settling over him slowly, standing in that room, that his father had not been mad. He had been desperately, urgently trying to leave a message. Every defaced crest, every warning scrawled in the margins, every incoherent page had been a man racing against his own fracturing mind to build a wall between his son and this place.

The message was clear, now that [[Zephyrix Skydelver|Zeph]] was standing exactly where his father had not wanted him to go.

_Do not go further._
