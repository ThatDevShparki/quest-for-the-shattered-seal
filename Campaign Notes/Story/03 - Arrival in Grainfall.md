# Arrival in Grainfall

At the edge of the forest just outside [[Campaign Knowledge/Locations/Grainfall]] stood a structure the locals called [[Campaign Knowledge/Locations/The Ruined Observatory]]. [[Campaign Knowledge/Characters/Zephyrix Skydelver|Zeph]] found it while orienting himself in the area, cross referencing his father's notes against the local geography. It sat where the treeline began, neither fully part of the town nor fully part of the forest. The stonework was old and purposeful. The decay was natural rather than violent.

Someone was living on the upper levels. He did not see them. He saw the evidence of them, the specific accumulation of signs his father had taught him to read before touching anything in a site. Occupation leaves a texture that differs from abandonment. The upper levels had that texture.

Going further inside, he found a tapestry.

Old, but sheltered from the light and weather that would have destroyed lesser fabric centuries ago. It hung across most of a wall in one of the lower chambers, its colors still vivid enough to read. And there, rendered in careful detail across the woven surface, was the crest from his mother's amulet. The same symbol his father had drawn and defaced in the final pages of [[Campaign Knowledge/Items/Aeralon's Journal|the journal]]. His family's mark, in a building he had never visited, at the edge of a town his father had spent years refusing to explain.

But the tapestry showed him something the defaced journal pages and the small pendant could not. What he had taken for a single symbol was in fact [[Campaign Knowledge/Clues/The Four Symbols|four]]. Each remarkably similar to the others but with minor deliberate variations between them. Not the natural drift of repeated rendering by different hands over time. Designed. Intentional. Four variations of the same crest, arranged together as though they were part of a set. Siblings rather than copies.

He took out [[Campaign Knowledge/Items/Aeralon's Journal|the journal]].

He opened it to one of the blank pages near the back. His hands hovered. Every mark in this journal had been made by his father's hand. The meticulous early notes, the careful sketches, the steady handwriting that held together for decades before fracturing in the final pages. [[Campaign Knowledge/Characters/Aeralon Ashdelve|Aeralon's]] entire career lived in these pages. His mind. His eye. His way of looking at the world and recording what he found.

[[Campaign Knowledge/Characters/Zephyrix Skydelver|Zeph]] pressed his pen to the page and drew.

He copied the four symbols carefully, the way his father had taught him to copy anything found in the field. Precision first. Interpretation later. His hand was steadier than [[Campaign Knowledge/Characters/Aeralon Ashdelve|Aeralon's]] had been at the end, and the four crests took shape on the page with the clean deliberate lines of someone trained to record exactly what he saw.

It was the first thing he had ever added to his father's journal. He noticed this as he finished the last symbol. Not as sentiment. As fact. The journal had been his father's most guarded possession, the thing that contained the lifetime of work [[Campaign Knowledge/Characters/Zephyrix Skydelver|Zeph]] had never been allowed to see. Now his marks were in it. His hand next to his father's. Continuing something.

He closed the page and went searching.

With the four symbols fresh, he worked through the journal looking for connections. In the earlier entries, the ones written in [[Campaign Knowledge/Characters/Aeralon Ashdelve|Aeralon's]] steady hand from before the deterioration, he found them. Precise descriptions of a ceremonial space beneath [[Campaign Knowledge/Locations/Grainfall]], cross referenced with a ritual his father had transcribed in full, complete with phonetic annotations for words in a language Aeralon could not identify. The symbols on the tapestry in front of him, the ritual in the journal, the ruin beneath the town. A trail his father had left without meaning to.

He moved on toward [[Campaign Knowledge/Locations/The Sealed Ruin Beneath Grainfall|the ruin]], because the journal was pulling him there and he now understood why.

---

[[Campaign Knowledge/Locations/The Sealed Ruin Beneath Grainfall|The ruin]] had been sealed. Officially. Recently. The stated reason was structural instability. Nothing in his father's survey notes supported this. [[Campaign Knowledge/Characters/Zephyrix Skydelver|Zeph]] went in anyway.

The air inside was still, old and undisturbed, carrying the faint mineral smell of excavated stone that he had known since childhood. The dust lay undisturbed across every surface. No foot had moved through this space in a very long time. He moved slowly. His father taught him to move slowly. You read a site before you touch it.

What he read was unfamiliar. He recognized this immediately. He had spent his life in [[Campaign Knowledge/Characters/Aeralon Ashdelve|Aeralon's]] orbit, surrounded by the breadth of what academic archaeology had documented. He had encountered obscure civilizations, regional cultures, poorly documented periods of history. Nothing in this ruin matched anything he had encountered in any of that. The civilization that built this place had left no trace in any record he could access.

Crypts lined the hallways. Skeletons in armor from centuries past, jeweled and armed in designs no living scholar had ever documented firsthand. And on every wall, in every hallway and chamber, murals. Detailed and deliberate, describing events and people and moments from a civilization that history had swallowed whole. He could not read them. The figures and events depicted belonged to a visual language as unfamiliar as [[Campaign Knowledge/Clues/Sae-th]].

He found [[Campaign Knowledge/Locations/The Sealed Ruin Beneath Grainfall|the ceremonial room]] deep inside. The hallways led to it with the architectural logic of a space designed to be arrived at rather than stumbled upon. He knew this before he entered. His father's journal had described this approach, every corridor and turn documented with the precision of the earlier years.

It was large. His footsteps changed quality when he entered, the echo deepening in the way of spaces built to hold something significant. Braziers and altars arranged on a central platform, the configuration matching the journal entries he had found in [[Campaign Knowledge/Locations/The Ruined Observatory|the Observatory]]. He was standing in his father's notes. Every detail confirmed.

[[Campaign Knowledge/Characters/Aeralon Ashdelve|Aeralon]] had seen this. Aeralon had stood here. And whatever he had intended to do in this room, whatever the ritual in the journal demanded, he had not finished it.

The walls told lineage. A family tree rendered in careful detail across the full circumference of the room, tracing bloodlines outward from a central ancestral point, branching into the complexity of generations upon generations of descent.

And there, woven into that web, was the crest.

Not a surprise. He had been following it here since the cold house, since the defaced pages and the amulet and the tapestry in [[Campaign Knowledge/Locations/The Ruined Observatory|the Observatory]]. But seeing it carved into stone on the wall of a civilization that predated every record he had ever encountered was different from seeing it on a pendant or in a journal. The scale of it. The age. [[Campaign Knowledge/Items/Sylara's Amulet|The amulet]] against his chest bore the same mark that this room had borne for centuries before his mother was born. Before her mother was born. Before the [[Campaign Knowledge/Organizations/The Dawnmere Lineage|Dawnmere]] name existed to be spoken.

His mother knew exactly where she came from. She wore the mark anyway. She chose light anyway.

The defaced crests in the final pages made sense now. But not the way he had expected.

Every version [[Campaign Knowledge/Characters/Aeralon Ashdelve|his father]] had drawn, the ones [[Campaign Knowledge/Characters/Zephyrix Skydelver|Zeph]] had seen in the cold house without understanding, matched a version on the wall in front of him. His father had not been defacing the symbol. He had been recording this wall. The crossed lines and heavy scratches that Zeph had read as fury were a fracturing hand trying to copy what it saw, each attempt less steady than the last, each line a little further from the precision that had defined Aeralon's career.

His father had stood in this room and done what he had always done in every ruin of his life. He had tried to take notes.

The scribbles surrounding the crests, the ones Zeph had dismissed as incoherent, were annotations. References to old gods and to a language called [[Campaign Knowledge/Clues/Sae-th]] and to something ancient connected to this place and [[Campaign Knowledge/Organizations/The Dawnmere Lineage|this bloodline]]. Not ravings. Field notes, made by a man losing his grip on the instrument in his hand but not on what the instrument was for.

It was not the crest on the wall that disturbed him. It was the realization settling over him slowly, standing in that room, that his father had been sane. More sane than [[Campaign Knowledge/Characters/Zephyrix Skydelver|Zeph]] had allowed himself to believe during the long years of watching. [[Campaign Knowledge/Characters/Aeralon Ashdelve|Aeralon]] had been working this entire time. Still the scholar. Still trying to leave behind something that could be read by someone who came after.

Which meant the ritual transcribed in the earlier pages was not the product of a deteriorating mind. It was field work. Sound scholarship from before the fracture. And it pointed at the platform in the center of this room and the braziers arranged upon it and the ritual his father had never performed.

[[Campaign Knowledge/Characters/Zephyrix Skydelver|Zeph]] was standing exactly where his father had not been able to finish.

_Do not go further._
