---

## Why It Matters

The Architect is the figure who appeared at Sylara's bedside the night before she died. He is the earliest mystery in Zeph's life -- a stranger who gave a title instead of a name and smiled warmly at a child who should not have been awake. Whether he caused her death, witnessed it, or was there for something else entirely, he is a thread that connects to everything that followed.

---

## What He Knows So Far

[[Zephyrix Skydelver|Zeph]] saw [[The Architect]] once as a child, at [[Sylara Dawnmere|Sylara]]'s bedside the night before she died. The man was warm, smiled, and when asked his name said only that he was "the Architect." Nothing else is known about him -- not his nature, his purpose, or whether he has appeared since.
