## Why It Matters

Aeralon is the bridge between what Sylara protected and what Zeph now faces. His descent traces the path from knowledge to obsession to ruin, and understanding what happened to him is essential to understanding what the ruin does to those who enter it and what Zeph risks by following the same trail.

---

## What He Knows So Far

[[Aeralon Ashdelve|Aeralon]] was his father, an arcanist and member of [[The Orryn Society]]. He became obsessed with [[Grainfall]] and [[The Sealed Ruin Beneath Grainfall|the sealed ruin beneath it]] after [[Sylara Dawnmere|Sylara]]'s death. He changed over the years in ways consistent with the Dreamrot. He left behind [[Aeralon's Journal|a journal]] that contained [[Ari]] and instructions from [[Sylara Dawnmere|Sylara]] on the first page. [[Brenvaal]] knew him and may know more about his final years.
