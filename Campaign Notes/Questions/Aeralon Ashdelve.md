## Why It Matters

Aeralon is the bridge between what Sylara protected and what Zeph now faces. His descent traces the path from knowledge to obsession to ruin, and understanding what happened to him is essential to understanding what the ruin does to those who enter it and what Zeph risks by following the same trail.

---

## What He Knows So Far

[[Campaign Knowledge/Characters/Aeralon Ashdelve|Aeralon]] was his father, an arcanist and member of [[Campaign Knowledge/Organizations/The Orryn Society]]. He became obsessed with [[Campaign Knowledge/Locations/Grainfall]] and [[Campaign Knowledge/Locations/The Sealed Ruin Beneath Grainfall|the sealed ruin beneath it]] after [[Campaign Knowledge/Characters/Sylara Dawnmere|Sylara]]'s death. He changed over the years in ways consistent with the Dreamrot. He left behind [[Campaign Knowledge/Items/Aeralon's Journal|a journal]] that contained [[Campaign Knowledge/Characters/Ari]] and instructions from [[Campaign Knowledge/Characters/Sylara Dawnmere|Sylara]] on the first page. [[Campaign Knowledge/Characters/Brenvaal]] knew him and may know more about his final years.
