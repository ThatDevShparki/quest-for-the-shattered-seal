# Questions -- Sae-th

### High Priority

- What do the passages in [[Campaign Knowledge/Items/Aeralon's Journal|Aeralon's journal]] actually say?

- Did [[Campaign Knowledge/Characters/Aeralon Ashdelve|Aeralon]]'s attempt to decode [[Campaign Knowledge/Clues/Sae-th|the language]] contribute to what happened to him?

### Medium Priority

- Is [[Campaign Knowledge/Clues/Sae-th|the language]] still spoken or written anywhere in the world?
- What does it mean for a language to bind the world? Is the binding intact?
- Are [[Campaign Knowledge/Clues/The Four Symbols|the four symbols]] characters from [[Campaign Knowledge/Clues/Sae-th|the old language]] or related to it?

## Why It Matters

Sae-th appears to be more than a dead language. If it once bound the world together, understanding it may be the key to understanding what is unraveling now and what Zeph's bloodline has to do with it.

---

## What He Knows So Far

[[Campaign Knowledge/Characters/Aeralon Ashdelve|Aeralon]] found passages written in [[Campaign Knowledge/Clues/Sae-th|the language]] and attempted to decode them. His journal contains partial translations, but much of the text remains unread. The language is connected to [[Campaign Knowledge/Clues/The Brothers|the Brothers]] and possibly to [[Campaign Knowledge/Clues/The Four Symbols|the symbols]] found in the ruin.
