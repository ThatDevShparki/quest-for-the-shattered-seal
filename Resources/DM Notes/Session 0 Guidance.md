
## 1. The Pitch

- Dark, serious tone
- Mystery-first and combat-first
- Exploration and slow reveals

> “This is not a heroic power fantasy.  
> This is a story about uncovering something you probably shouldn’t.”


---

## 2. Themes 

- Forbidden knowledge
- Loss of humanity
- Becoming what you fear
- Moral compromise
- Slow corruption

---

## 3. Character Rules 

### REQUIRED:

- Your character is **grounded and serious**
- You have a reason to explore ruins or investigate the unknown
- You are willing to engage with mystery

### Home-Rules

- Materials are optional for casting however if I see abuse I will have your arcane focus break (revivify 4 times in a row)
- Nat 20 initiative allows you to choose where you want to be
- Drinking potion as action grants full benefit, Or action to drink and get a free dodge action

---

## 4. The Big Hook (Shared Backstory)


> “We all know each other before the campaign began, and find ourselves in Grainfall”


**1. Why did you go into the ruin?**  
(greed, curiosity, orders, desperation)

**2. What did you see that disturbed you?**  
(give them narrative control)

**3. What did you do that got you arrested?**

---

## 5. Dreamrot

Explain clearly:

- You are infected
- It will change over time
- It is both a curse and something else
- It is central to the story


> “Your characters will change over the campaign. Possibly in irreversible ways.”
> 
---

## 6. The Most Important Question


> “What would your character do if they discovered something that could change the world—but at a cost to themselves?”


---

# The Shared Rumor Exercise

- “The ruins were built to worship something beneath the world.”
- “The ancient civilization was destroyed by divine punishment.”
- “People who enter the ruins don’t come back… or come back wrong.”
